## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — Five Key Takeaways

**1. The Melaka PRN is imminent (1–3 months) and the coalition map has been fundamentally rewritten.** The state election is due on or before November 2026, with the Chief Minister having disclosed a 120-day window on 17 May 2026 and narrowed the signal to "possibly September" in June 2026. The SPR Melaka office posted supplementary electoral-roll verification on 3 August 2026 — direct evidence of active pre-election preparation. The BN-PH state coalition government has **collapsed**: PH withdrew 14–16 July 2026 over a BN+PN constitutional amendment creating 7 unelected assembly seats (passed 23-5, defying PM Anwar). The next PRN will be a **four-way contest**: BN (with informal PAS backing) as front-runner, a weakened DAP-led PH now in opposition, a moribund BERSATU isolated within a PAS-run PN, and BERSAMA (Rafizi Ramli's new party) as a vote-splitter risk.

**2. A de facto Muafakat Nasional reactivation has been validated by consecutive state election landslides.** Informal UMNO-PAS cooperation — analysts describe it as "similar to former Muafakat Nasional alliance" — delivered BN 48/56 seats in Johor (11 Jul 2026) and 25/36 in Negeri Sembilan (1 Aug 2026), with PAS publicly directing votes to BN. This vindicates Dr Akmal Saleh's advocacy for reviving the UMNO-PAS pact, achieved *informally* despite UMNO President Zahid's *formal* rejection. BERSATU was wiped out in all three recent state elections (Sabah Nov 2025, Johor, NS) and PAS severed cooperation with BERSATU on 8-9 June 2026. The PN electoral machine is now entirely PAS-run.

**3. The Akmal-Zahid policy split is the engine of national UMNO rhetoric and has intensified the federal-state posture divergence.** The documented split (Akmal proposed UMNO exit the unity government + revive Muafakat; Zahid rejected both) drives Akmal's national rhetoric trajectory — 3R red lines, boycott campaigns, and the characterisation of DAP's political culture as difficult to reform. Akmal's selective EXCO resignation (19 Jan 2026) decoupled his rhetoric from state-coalition constraint. This split is now sharpened: BN cooperates with PH federally (under escalating strain, with DAP putting its government role to a vote on 4 Aug 2026) but competes *against* PH at state level (Johor, NS, now Melaka).

**4. Two verification gates were closed and one high-priority signal remains unverified.** Shamsul Iskandar's political re-entry is **confirmed blocked** (no elected office, resigned government post Nov 2025, four corruption charges Dec 2025). Bakri Jamaluddin's PN/PAS affiliation is **confirmed** (Tangga Batu MP, 40.65%, majority 8,849). The highest-priority unverified signal is a report that Mas Ermieyati Samsudin's BERSATU membership has been **suspended since 2026** — appearing only in English Wikipedia, absent from Malay Wikipedia, and uncorroborated by news sources or the BERSATU Wikipedia page. If confirmed, it creates a major institutional anomaly (a suspended-party member chairing the PAC) and materially alters her electoral viability.

**5. The structural Melaka defence nexus is proven — and it is Army, not Navy.** The PIR's original framing assumed a TLDM (navy) nexus. Evidence corrects this: TLDM headquarters is at Lumut, Perak. The actual structural nexus is **land and airborne** — the Malaysian Army's 3rd Division and the 10th Parachute Brigade are both headquartered in Malacca, creating a direct minister-to-local-base structural link for Deputy Defence Minister Adly Zahari, whose political base (Alor Gajah/Bukit Katil) overlaps the military concentration exactly. MINDEF's 2026 budget is RM21.746 billion.
