## 4. Critical Findings

### 4.1 PIR-05 (CRITICAL): Melaka PRN Imminent — Coalition Map Rewritten

**This is the single most important intelligence deliverable of the inaugural cycle.**

The Melaka state election is **1-3 months away** (due ≤November 2026, possibly September; CM disclosed a 120-day window on 17 May 2026). The coalition configuration has fundamentally changed since the 2021 baseline:

- **The Melaka BN-PH state government coalition has COLLAPSED.** PH withdrew 14-16 July 2026 over a BN+PN constitutional amendment creating **7 unelected assembly seats**, passed 23-5 (all 21 BN + 2 PN vs all 5 PH), defying PM Anwar's plea. The next PRN will be contested with **PH in opposition**, not in coalition with BN.

- **The 7-unelected-seats amendment is a coalition-engineering tool.** With 23 elected BN+PN votes + 7 appointable seats, the BN+PN bloc can command up to 30 of 35 (expanded) seats regardless of the elected result — a structural lock-in that marginalises PH (5 elected) and entrenches the BN+PN working relationship. This is the most consequential governance/coalition development in Melaka this year.

- **Current assembly composition:** BN 20 (UMNO 17, MCA 2, MIC 1) / PH 5 (DAP 4, AMANAH 1) / PN 3 (BERSATU 1, PAS 1, WAWASAN 1). BN has a two-thirds majority alone (20 of 28).

- **Informal BN-PAS pact (de facto Muafakat reactivation):** In Johor (48/56 BN landslide) and Negeri Sembilan (25/36), PAS publicly directed votes to BN; analysts describe it as "similar to former Muafakat Nasional alliance." This is Akmal's Muafakat vision achieved *de facto* at state level, despite Zahid blocking it *formally* at federal level.

- **BERSAMA (Rafizi Ramli's new party) is explicitly targeting the Melaka PRN** and has rejected coalition talks — creating a **four-way contest** and a vote-splitter risk for the anti-BN vote. Rafizi withdrew BERSAMA from NS "to focus on the Malacca election."

- **PAS cut political cooperation with BERSATU (8-9 June 2026).** BERSATU was wiped out in three consecutive state elections (Sabah Nov 2025, Johor Jul 2026, NS Aug 2026). The PN electoral machine is now PAS-run (chairman Samsuri, elections director Sanusi, sec-gen Takiyuddin — all PAS).

**Assessment:** The next Melaka PRN will almost certainly replicate the Johor/NS template: BN dominant, PAS tactically backing BN in Malay seats, BERSATU marginalised, PH weakened, and BERSAMA splitting the reformist vote. The federal/state divergence is the defining structural feature — BN cooperates with PH federally (under strain, with DAP now questioning its government role) but competes against PH at state level.

This is not a minor installation: a divisional HQ plus a strategic airborne brigade in a single state is a substantial military concentration.

The geographic overlap with Adly Zahari's political base is exact — he is MP for Alor Gajah and MLA for Bukit Katil, both in Malacca. As Deputy Minister of Defence, Adly is the federal political overseer of the ministry whose premier Peninsula land/airborne formations are headquartered in his home state. ### BN/PH/PN Dynamics

- **Federal level:** BN (Zahid) remains in the unity government with PH (reaffirmed 30 Apr 2026 during NS crisis). DAP now questioning whether to stay (CEC vote, 4 Aug). Strain is real and escalating.
- **State level (Melaka):** BN-PH coalition **collapsed** (PH withdrew Jul 2026). Next PRN is BN(+PAS) vs PH.
- **State level (Johor/NS):** BN wins *against* PH, with PAS backing. The winning formula is BN+PAS, not BN+PH.
- **Federal/state divergence:** BN cooperates with PH federally (under strain) but competes against PH at state level. This is the defining structural feature and bears directly on Ab Rauf's autonomy (PIR-06) — he runs a state BN that has shed its PH partner while his federal president remains in cabinet with PH.

### The PAS-BERSATU Realignment

PAS holds **2 of PN's 3 Malacca federal seats** (Zulkifli/Jasin, Bakri/Tangga Batu) and 1 of 3 state seats (Jailani/Rembia). BERSATU holds 1 state seat (Sungai Udang) and 1 MP (Mas Ermieyati/Masjid Tanah). The PN electoral machine is entirely PAS-run. The viable Malay-Muslim opposition/right bloc is **UMNO + PAS (+ WAWASAN)**, with BERSATU excluded. Muafakat Nasional is technically an NGO (since 15 Dec 2022), with its Malacca chairman (Muhammad Jailani Khamis) now a sitting PN-PAS ADUN — a person-level UMNO-PAS realignment nexus.
