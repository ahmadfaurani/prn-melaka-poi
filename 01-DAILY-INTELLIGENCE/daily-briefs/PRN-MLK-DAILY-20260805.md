## 5. Coalition Dynamics Update

### The Four-Way Melaka PRN Contest — "BN Plus" Activates

Ab Rauf's "BN Plus" statement (2 Aug 2026) is the defining coalition development since the inaugural brief. It signals that BN's coalition strategy for the imminent PRN is an **open-negotiation posture** — welcoming cooperation with "any party" under a "BN Plus" formula, while not being "bound by any arrangement that has yet to be formally established." This is simultaneously a seat-defense strategy (Ab Rauf himself holds N02 Tanjung Bidara, margin 364) and a coalition-arithmetic decision. The NS template (BN+PN government, 25/36) is the projected base case.

1. **BN (+ informal PAS backing)** — Incumbent, 20 seats, front-runner. "BN Plus" posture signals open negotiations. Shed PH partner (Jul 2026). Engineered 7-unelected-seats lock-in with PN. Informal Muafakat reactivation delivering Johor/NS landslides. Ab Rauf as CM/defender. Tok Mat as federal elections strategist. PAS Melaka machinery (Election Director Ir Mohd Hanim Abas) mobilizing via Muafakat BN-PN framework.
2. **PH (DAP-led, weakened)** — 5 seats, now in state opposition. Adly-led withdrawal defying Anwar. AMANAH electorally marginal (0 in NS, 1 in Melaka). Non-Malay shift away from DAP (Johor, Sabah). DAP internally questioning government role (CEC vote, 4 Aug 2026). Anthony Loke resigned NS DAP chief. **Fracture risk: LOW–MODERATE and latent** — the BN-PH fracture is confirmed; the forward-looking risk is further fragmentation within the opposition bloc.
