### 4.2 Mas Ermieyati's Suspension is Factional, Not Ideological (PIR-09 — Keystone)

The FMT article (B1) reveals that Mas Ermieyati's BERSATU suspension is **factional**, not policy-based:

- **The factional split:** Hamzah Zainudin was sacked from BERSATU on 13 February 2026 (per cycle 1). Mas Ermieyati and VP Ronald Kiandee then attended Hamzah's 'Reset' program AND met DPM Zahid Hamidi in Bangkok. The BERSATU discipline board (under president Muhyiddin's faction) punished them for this factional disloyalty.
- **The WAWASAN connection:** Hamzah formed WAWASAN on 13 June 2026 (cycle 1). WAWASAN now holds 1 Melaka state seat (N24 Bemban, confirmed this cycle). Mas Ermieyati was punished for supporting the Hamzah faction — the very faction that became WAWASAN. **Her most logical defection path is to WAWASAN**, where she would rejoin Hamzah's camp and potentially contest under a WAWASAN-PN ticket.
- **Electoral vulnerability:** Her Masjid Tanah margin narrowed from 16,200 (2013) to 4,411 (2022). As a suspended BERSATU member atop a party wiped out in 3 state elections, her seat is now imperilled. Her options: (a) remain in PN as a BERSATU figurehead (electorally weak, party positions stripped), (b) defect to WAWASAN (Hamzah's party — logical factional alignment), (c) contest as independent, or (d) accommodation with BN/PAS (the winning Melaka formula).
- **PAC chairmanship unaffected:** Her PAC chair (parliamentary appointment by Anwar) and MP seat (elected) are not party-controlled — they survive the BERSATU suspension. Notably, she was nominated by Anwar — the same Anwar whose DPM (Zahid) she met in Bangkok, the meeting that triggered her suspension. This suggests back-channel BN-PH-Mas Ermieyati communication.

### 4.3 BERSAMA Launches This Week — The Four-Way Contest is Live (PIR-05)

BERSAMA's Melaka ground operations launch on **8 August 2026 (this Saturday)** — 3 days after this collection — with two programs at Sentral Melaka Hotel (Rafizi Facebook). This is not theoretical; it is happening now. Combined with the Utusan/MalaysiaPost confirmations (candidates by early August, solo, no coalition), the four-way Melaka contest is live:

1. **BN** (+ informal PAS backing) — 20 seats, front-runner, "BN Plus" open to negotiation

### 4.4 Adly Zahari's Ironic Reversal — The Appointed-ADUN Boomerang (PIR-10)

This cycle documents a remarkable irony in Adly Zahari's coalition positioning:

- **April 2026 (FMT, B4):** Adly PROPOSES the appointed-ADUN mechanism as a cooperation formula with BN — a goodwill gesture to avoid three-cornered fights and ensure post-election stability.
- **July 2026 (Wikipedia keystone, B6):** The BN+PN bloc PASSES the appointed-ADUN constitutional amendment (23-5) — the very mechanism Adly proposed — but WITHOUT PH's input, as a coalition-engineering tool to lock in BN+PN dominance (23 elected + 7 appointable = 30 of 35). PH withdraws in protest.
- **The boomerang:** Adly's own proposed cooperation mechanism was weaponized against PH. He proposed it as a bridge; BN+PN turned it into a wall. This is the defining irony of Melaka's 2026 coalition dynamics.

Adly's subsequent posture (August, B5) is **defensive and nationalized** — "value-based politics, integrity, governing capability" — spoken at a Penang Amanah convention, not a Melaka event. His national Amanah VP role (and federal Deputy Defence Minister portfolio, serving in Anwar's cabinet) appears to be overtaking his Melaka PH chair role. The PH meeting on 8 August (same day as BERSAMA's Melaka launch) will reveal whether PH develops a Melaka-specific strategy or remains in post-defeat muhasabah (self-reflection) mode.

### 4.5 "BN Plus" — The Open-Door Posture Before the Doors Close (PIR-05/06)

Ab Rauf's "BN Plus" statement (B7, 2 August) is BN's official pre-election coalition posture — **open to negotiation with anyone, but not bound to any unformalised coalition**. The key signal:

- **Zahid explicitly cited the NS BN+PN cooperation as a "catalyst" for PRN Melaka momentum** — telegraphing that BN's Melaka strategy is to replicate the NS/Johor informal BN-PAS/PN pact.
- **Ab Rauf's "not bound" language** preserves flexibility: BN can negotiate with PN (PAS) for Malay seats, leave urban seats for MCA, and potentially absorb independents/defectors (the "Plus" in BN Plus).
- **The NS template:** BN 18 + PN 7 = 25/36 (two-thirds). In Melaka, BN 20 + PN 3 = 23/28 (already a majority). The "BN Plus" formula + 7 appointable seats = structural lock-in regardless of elected results.
