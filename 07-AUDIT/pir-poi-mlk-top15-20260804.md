## Collection Protocol

1. **Cronjob execution:** CJ-MLK-01 through CJ-MLK-04 execute on schedule, each reading auto-approve suggestions from previous cycle
2. **Raw collection:** Articles saved to `04-DATA-AND-SOURCES/raw-scrapes/YYYYMMDD/` with PIR tags
3. **Entity extraction:** Entities extracted to `04-DATA-AND-SOURCES/processed-entities/YYYYMMDD/`
4. **Sentiment analysis:** Sentiment analyzed to `04-DATA-AND-SOURCES/sentiment-analysis/YYYYMMDD/`
5. **Daily brief:** CJ-MLK-05 synthesizes all collection, tracks PIR resolution status, delivers to Director
6. **Auto-approve loop:** Each cronjob writes TOP 3 suggestions to `07-AUDIT/top3-mlk-suggestions-CJMLK0X.md`
7. **Git sync:** CJ-MLK-06 commits and pushes daily
