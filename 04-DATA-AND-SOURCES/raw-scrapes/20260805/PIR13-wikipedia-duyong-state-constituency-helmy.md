### Key Trends
- **Competitive/marginal seat:** margins narrow in multiple elections (200 in 2021, 205 in 2013).
- BN held 1995–2013 (Gan family, MCA); DAP/PR/PH 2013–2021; BN (UMNO) regained 2021.
- **Gan family dominance:** Gan Boon Leong + Gan Tian Loo (BN/MCA) represented ~18 years (1995–2013).
- **PN emergence:** PN captured 23.65% in 2021, a significant new factor splitting opposition vote.
- Declining turnout: 94.85% (2013) → 68.19% (2021).

### Mohd Noor Helmy — marginal-seat ADUN, first UMNO holder of former-MCA seat (PIR-13 / cross-ref PIR-27)
- **Mohd Noor Helmy Abdul Halem won Duyong (N21) in 2021 by just 200 votes (1.65%)** — making this one of the 5 most marginal seats in Malacca (cross-ref PIR-27 marginal-seat defence strategy). His retention of the seat — and therefore his deputy-EXCO role — is acutely at risk in the imminent PRN (due ≤Nov 2026).
- **First UMNO ADUN for a former-MCA seat.** Duyong was an MCA stronghold 1995-2013 (the Gan family, two generations). The seat then went DAP (2013-2021) before Mohd Noor Helmy (UMNO) won it for BN in 2021. This is a Malay-seat consolidation within BN: a 61.4%-Malay seat that MCA could no longer hold was taken by UMNO. This shift may carry latent MCA-UMNO seat-allocation friction heading into the PRN (cross-ref PIR-20, Ngwe Hee Sem / MCA representation).
- The 2021 three-way split (BN 38.55% / PH 36.90% / PN 23.65%) shows PN is a real third force here. If PN's 23.65% holds or grows, and the PH vote consolidates, Mohd Noor Helmy's 200-vote cushion could evaporate. This is a classic three-cornered marginal.

### Demographics & defensibility
- 61.4% Malay / 33.3% Chinese — a mixed seat that leans Malay. UMNO's win here reflects Malay-belt consolidation; the 33% Chinese bloc is the swing that DAP will target to retake. The seat's competitiveness (narrow margins since 2013) makes it a priority defensive seat for BN and a priority offensive target for PH/DAP.
