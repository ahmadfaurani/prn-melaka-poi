## Full Content (Wikipedia extraction)

### Overview
The **Ministry of Defence** (Malay: *Kementerian Pertahanan*; Jawi: كمنترين ڤرتاهنن), abbreviated **MINDEF** or **KEMENTAH**, is the Malaysian government ministry responsible for: Defence, national security, army, navy, air force, armed forces; Hydrography, intelligence services, counterintelligence, military intelligence; National service, veterans affairs.

### Key Facts
- **Formed:** 31 August 1957
- **Headquarters:** Wisma Pertahanan, Jalan Padang Tembak, 50634 Kuala Lumpur
- **Employees:** 168,774 (2017)
- **Annual Budget:** **MYR 21,746,268,300 (2026)** ← current-year budget figure
- **Website:** www.mod.gov.my
