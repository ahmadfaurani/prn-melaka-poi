### Background
- Pre-election govt formed after 2023 Sabah crisis: UMNO tried to withdraw support from Hajiji, failed → UMNO and BERSATU excluded from GRS; PH brought into state govt instead
- "Sabah for Sabahans" sentiment dominated
- Mining license corruption scandal (Albert Tei secret recordings)
- GRS and PH agreed to an electoral pact (avoid contesting each other)
- STAR and SAPP left GRS (2 Oct 2025) over GRS-PH pact; UPKO exited PH (10 Nov 2025)

### Aftermath
- Hajiji sworn in for second term (29 seats) with support from BN, UPKO, STAR, PH, KDM, 5 independents
- BN inclusion sparked controversy within GRS ranks
- STAR entered opposition

### Opinion Polls (final): GRS 25%, WARISAN 22%, BN 25%, PH 19%, PN 4%

## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-03)

1. **PH collapsed to 1 seat in Sabah (-5)** — the first of three consecutive state-election humiliations for PH (Sabah Nov 2025 → Johor Jul 2026 → NS Aug 2026). The Johor page explicitly cited "similar pattern seen in 2025 Sabah state election where DAP lost all contested seats." This is the documented national trend that frames Adly Zahari's coalition positioning (PIR-10): PH's electoral viability outside DAP urban bastions is in severe decline.

2. **PN: 1 seat (+1) in Sabah** — minimal. BERSATU excluded from GRS; PN marginal. Consistent with BERSATU's wipeout pattern (the party was excluded from the Sabah governing coalition after 2023). For Mas Ermieyati (PIR-09), this is another data point that the PN/BERSATU electoral vehicle is weak at state level nationwide.

3. **BN: 6 seats (-5) in Sabah** — BN also declined here, but BN recovered dramatically in Johor (+8 → 48) and NS (18 seats, led by Tok Mat). So BN's trajectory diverges by region: weak in East Malaysia, resurgent in Peninsula Malay heartlands (Johor, NS) — the pattern most relevant to Melaka (a Peninsula state).

4. **GRS-PH electoral pact** in Sabah is an example of cross-coalition seat negotiation (avoiding contests) — the kind of "seat negotiations among opposition parties expected to be critical" flagged for the Melaka PRN. The Melaka analogue will be whether BERSAMA (Rafizi) negotiates with PH or contests alone (Rafizi "rejected coalition talks" in Johor).
