## Key Summary

Umno Youth chief **Datuk Dr Muhamad Akmal Saleh** dismissed allegations of being anti-Chinese, clarifying that his disagreements with **DAP** are political and policy-based, not racially motivated. Statements made on the **Keluar Sekejap podcast** with **Khairy Jamaluddin** (former Umno Youth chief).

## Key Quotes

> "When we are against DAP, we are against its policies. It has nothing to do with the Chinese race. My mother-in-law is Chinese."

> "Of course. It has a chauvinist DNA. It exists." *(on whether DAP still harbours a chauvinistic streak)*

> "The issues I raise, I don't attack. I defend. I don't start the fight — I defend."

> "I didn't attack — I defended."

## Main Points

### Position on DAP
- **Opposition is policy-based**, not racial
- Believes DAP still has a **"chauvinist DNA"**
- Acknowledged some DAP leaders are **more moderate and accommodating**, specifically naming:
  - **Anthony Loke** (DAP secretary-general)
  - **Steven Sim** (DAP deputy secretary-general)
- Believes DAP **could evolve** but change would take **10–20 years**

### Willingness to Cooperate
- Prepared to work with DAP representatives in government **if sensitive matters are handled carefully**
- Cited working relationship with a **DAP deputy exco member in Melaka**, whom he has defended on several occasions

### Defense of Combative Approach
Akmal insisted he **responds rather than initiates** disputes, citing these controversies as examples where he "defended":
- **Inverted national flag incident**
- **'Allah' socks issue**
- **Recognition of the Unified Examination Certificate (UEC)**
- **Kampung Baru redevelopment**

## Context

- Interview occurred within the framework of Malaysia's **unity government** comprising PH and BN.
- Related articles suggest ongoing tensions between DAP and Umno youth wings over coalition loyalty.
- PH and BN youth wings reportedly **had not met** since the unity government was formed (as of June 2026).
