## Key Findings
1. Mas Ermieyati, as PAC Chairman, led a **working visit to the Malaysia-Thailand border in Kelantan** (Rantau Panjang) as part of the cooking oil subsidy investigation
2. PAC planned to **call more witnesses** on cooking oil subsidy management under KPDN before tabling to Dewan Rakyat
3. PAC found management of cooking oil price control and subsidies **unsatisfactory** on several matters
4. PAC found **2 companies without halal certificates** packing subsidised cooking oil
5. This proceeding culminated in **DR.27/2026** (tabled 16 Jul 2026) — the most recent 2026 PAC report
