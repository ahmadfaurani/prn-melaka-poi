## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-04)

### Zulkifli Ismail — MP status CONFIRMED (PIR-12)
- **Zulkifli Ismail is the current MP for Jasin (P139), PN/PAS, since 2022.** This is confirmed by Wikipedia's authoritative electoral data. He won GE15 2022 with 35.95% in a three-way split, majority only 322 votes (0.42%) — one of the narrowest margins in Malacca and a national-scale marginal seat.
- **Trajectory:** The Tangga Batu federal constituency page (separate scrape) records that **Zulkifli Ismail contested Tangga Batu in 2018 under PAS** and received 8,961 (12.96%), losing. He then won Jasin in 2022 under PN/PAS. This establishes him as a PAS loyalist who contested twice (2018 loss, 2022 win) before entering parliament.
- **PN GAIN from BN:** Jasin was a BN/UMNO stronghold 1974–2022 (held by former Deputy PM Abdul Ghafar Baba 1978–1995). Zulkifli's 2022 win is part of the national "Green Wave" (PN/PAS surge). The seat is Malay-majority (74.3%) — classic PAS target demographic.

### PAS Melaka commissioner title — UNCONFIRMED (PIR-12)
- The "PAS Melaka commissioner" title attributed to Zulkifli Ismail in the PIR registry is **NOT corroborated by Wikipedia.** The Jasin page confirms him as MP (PN/PAS) but does not mention any state party commissionership. The Malay Wikipedia "Zulkifli Ismail" page is a DIFFERENT person (a 1963-born actor from Alor Setar). The PAS EN-Wikipedia national page does not list state commissioners by name. **The commissionership remains an open verification item** requiring Malay-language news (harakahdaily, Sinar Harian) or PAS official sources — the web_search backend cannot retrieve these.

### Jasin's state-seat composition: 4 BN + 1 PN (PIR-12 / cross-ref PIR-27)
- Within Zulkifli's federal constituency, **4 of 5 state seats are BN (UMNO)** — Rim, Serkam, Merlimau (Akmal Saleh), Sungai Rambai — and only Bemban (N24) is PN (WAWASAN, Mohd Yadzil Yaakub). This means the PAS MP presides over a state-level base that is overwhelmingly BN. His federal seat is marginal (322 votes) and the state seats beneath it are mostly safe BN — PAS/PN's grassroots organisational depth in Jasin is thin beyond his personal federal win.
