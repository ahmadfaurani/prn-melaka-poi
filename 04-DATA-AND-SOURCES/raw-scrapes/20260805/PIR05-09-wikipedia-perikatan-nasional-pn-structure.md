## Full Content

### Overview

**Perikatan Nasional (PN)** (English: *National Alliance*) is a Malaysian political coalition, currently the **second largest in the Dewan Rakyat** with **68/222 seats** (behind Pakatan Harapan's 79). It is a **right-wing to far-right** coalition.

- **Founded:** 29 February 2020 | **Registered:** 7 August 2020
- **Founder:** Muhyiddin Yassin
- **Split from:** Pakatan Harapan (PH)
- **Headquarters:** Level SA-2, Menara RKT, No 36 Jalan Raja Abdullah, Kuala Lumpur
- **Colours:** Dark blue and white
- **Slogans:** *"Mengeratkan perpaduan"* (Strengthen unity); *"Bersih dan Stabil"* (Clean and Stable)
- **Newspaper:** Harakah

### Ideology & Political Position
- **Ideology:** Islamism, Islamic fundamentalism, religious conservatism, *Ketuanan Melayu*, religious nationalism, right-wing populism
- **Position:** Right-wing to far-right

## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-03)

1. **KEYSTONE — PAS–BERSATU break (8 June 2026):** The single most important 2026 coalition-dynamics signal. PAS (43 MPs) and BERSATU (19 MPs) — the two substantive PN components — broke *political cooperation* while both remaining inside the PN shell. This is a structural fracture within the opposition bloc and directly affects PIR-09: Mas Ermieyati Samsudin is a top BERSATU leader (Ketua Srikandi BERSATU, MP Masjid Tanah). A PAS–BERSATU fracture complicates PN's Melaka seat strategy (PAS and BERSATU fielded separate candidates in 2021 and may now be even less able to coordinate).

2. **PAS dominance of PN leadership:** Chairman (Samsuri), Sec-Gen (Takiyuddin), Elections Director (Sanusi), Youth Chief (Afnan Hamimi), Spokesperson (Annuar Musa) are ALL PAS. BERSATU holds only the Women Chief post (Nolee Ashilin). This means PN's electoral machine is now effectively PAS-run — relevant to how PN contests Melaka and whether a BERSATU figure (Mas Ermieyati) is retained as a marquee candidate.

3. **WAWASAN (Hamzah faction, 6 MPs):** A new component created 13 June 2026, days after the PAS–BERSATU break. Hamzah Zainudin — former Opposition Leader and former PN Sec-Gen — leads it. This is a factional reorganisation of the non-PAS bloc inside PN.

4. **Malacca: PN holds 3 of 6 federal seats.** Combined with the 2021 state result (PN 2/28 state seats, both BERSATU), this is the baseline for the next PRN.

5. **Opposition-leadership churn confirmed:** Hamzah → (Azmin as Sec-Gen Dec 2024) → Samsuri as Chairman Feb 2026.
