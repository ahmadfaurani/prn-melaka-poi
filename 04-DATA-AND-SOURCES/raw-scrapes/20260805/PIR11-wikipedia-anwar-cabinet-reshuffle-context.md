### Overview
The **Anwar Ibrahim cabinet** is the **23rd federal cabinet of Malaysia**, formed **3 December 2022** (composition announced 2 Dec 2022). Also known as "Unity Government" (Kerajaan Perpaduan) / "Madani government" (Kerajaan Madani). Holds a two-thirds majority coalition. PN declined to join, forming the opposition.

- **Legislature:** 15th Parliament
- **Monarchs:** Abdullah of Pahang (2022–2024); Ibrahim Iskandar of Johor (since 2024)
- **Total members:** 62 (32 ministers + 30 deputy ministers)
- **Opposition Leader:** Hamzah Zainudin (2022–2026), Ahmad Samsuri Mokhtar (2026), Hamzah Zainudin (2026–present) *(note: opposition leadership changed in 2026)*

## Collector Notes (cross-PIR context)
- **Dec 2023 reshuffle** set both Adly's and (eventually) Adam Adli's ministerial superiors: Khaled Nordin moved Higher Ed → Defence (Adly's boss); Zambry moved Foreign Affairs → Higher Ed (Adam Adli's boss from Dec 2025).
- **Dec 2025 reshuffle** moved Adam Adli: Deputy Youth & Sports → Deputy Higher Education (under Zambry). His old Youth & Sports deputy role went to Mordi Bimol (DAP); the ministry itself is now headed by Mohammed Taufiq Johari (PKR).
- **Cross-coalition deputy pairings:** Both Adly (PH-AMANAH deputy under BN-UMNO minister Khaled Nordin) and Adam Adli (PH-PKR deputy under BN-UMNO minister Zambry) serve as PH deputies beneath BN ministers — a structural feature of the "unity government" power-sharing that bears on their advancement (deputy roles under a different coalition's minister can constrain visibility/autonomy).
- **Opposition Leader changed in 2026:** Hamzah Zainudin → Ahmad Samsuri Mokhtar → Hamzah Zainudin. This opposition instability is the political environment in which Mas Ermieyati (PN/BERSATU, PAC chair) operates — and may relate to her reported BERSATU suspension.
