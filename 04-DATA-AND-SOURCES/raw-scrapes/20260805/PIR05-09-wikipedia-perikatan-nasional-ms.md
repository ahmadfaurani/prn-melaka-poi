## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-03)

1. **Opposition Leader = Hamzah Zainudin (WAWASAN).** This resolves the "Hamzah→Samsuri→Hamzah" ambiguity from CJ-MLK-02. The two churn tracks are: (a) **PN Chairman** (party post): Muhyiddin → Samsuri (22 Feb 2026); (b) **Ketua Pembangkang / Opposition Leader** (parliamentary post): Hamzah remains. The Malay page confirms Hamzah as current Opposition Leader while Samsuri is PN chairman. So it is NOT a round-trip; the two posts are held by different people simultaneously.

2. **Seat discrepancies (2022 vs current):** BM page shows BERSATU at 31 MP/58 DUN (2022 election figures) and WAWASAN at 6 MP/5 DUN; EN page shows BERSATU 19 MP/49 DUN (current, after by-election + Sabah + Johor + NS losses). The current figures (19 MP) are the operative ones for analysis.

3. **PAS dominance reconfirmed:** Chairman (Samsuri), Sec-Gen (Takiyuddin), Elections Director (Sanusi), Youth Chief (Afnan Hamimi), Spokesperson (Annuar Musa) = ALL PAS. BERSATU holds only Women's Chief (Nolee Ashilin). MIPP holds Treasurer. PN's electoral apparatus is PAS-run.

4. **"BERSATU, BERGERAK, SE-MALAYSIA" slogan since 21 March 2025** — the current PN campaign slogan.

5. **UMNO never recognised Ismail Sabri's govt as a "PN government"** — only "Kerajaan Malaysia." This is an old but telling indicator of UMNO-BERSATU ambivalence that has now hardened into the PAS-BERSATU break.

6. **Pembangkang negeri (state opposition): all states except Sarawak and Johor (no PN reps).** Melaka is a state-opposition position for PN (PN held 2/28 in 2021). Confirms PN's Melaka role = opposition to Ab Rauf's BN state govt.
