## Major Issues & Explanations

### 1. Detailed Design Rework (4,000 Items)
- LUNAS completed detailed design in August 2024
- Naval Group (France, license holder) mandated comprehensive review
- Review feedback received April 2025 (~8 months delay)
- Required rework of ~4,000 piping/cable configurations
- Additional 50 km of cabling for LCS 1 (total now ~300 km)
- Design and construction done concurrently ("tarik tali" approach) – abnormal for shipbuilding

> **Laksamana Franklin:** "Design sehingga Ogos 2024, usaha menyiapkan detailed design adalah usaha anak tempatan which is syarikat LUNAS... Kontrak mensyaratkan untuk detailed design ini diselesaikan dan diserah kepada pihak TLDM. Dalam proses-proses pembinaan yang lazim... design ini kena siap 100 peratus baru kita memulakan pembinaan. This is normal-lah, norm in any project. Tapi dalam kes LCS ini, disebabkan sejarah yang projek ini bawa, kita tak dapat melaksanakan proses ini sepenuhnya."

### 2. Remaining 17.16% Work Breakdown
Two main contributors:
1. Construction slowness – plateaued at ~3% for 8 months (expected to continue until March-April 2026)
2. Extensive recabling/repiping – delays in starting harbour acceptance trials
