### Overview
- Date: 20 Nov 2021 | Seats: 28 (15 for majority) | Registered voters: 495,195 | Turnout: 65.85%
- Snap election triggered by political crisis: 4 MLAs withdrew support from CM Sulaiman Md Ali on 4 Oct 2021.
- 4 defectors: Idris Haron (Sungai Udang) + Nor Azman Hassan (Pantai Kundor) from BN/UMNO; Norhizam Hassan Baktee (Pengkalan Batu) as independent; Noor Effandi Ahmad (Telok Mas) from PN/BERSATU.
- **First state election in Malay Peninsula since fall of PH federal govt (2020).** BN and PN — both in federal govt — COMPETED AGAINST EACH OTHER (previously only seen in East Malaysia).

## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-04)

### Duyong (N21) marginality confirmed (PIR-13 / PIR-27)
- Duyong is explicitly listed as a **marginal government seat (38.55%, <45% threshold)** in the 2021 analysis — corroborating the Duyong scrape (200-vote majority). Mohd Noor Helmy's seat is one of BN's most marginal. This is the structural constraint on his succession positioning: he must defend a 1.65%-margin seat in the imminent PRN.

### Tangga Batu & Jasin — 2021 BN lead, 2022 PN win (PIR-12/15)
- In the 2021 state election, **BN led in both Tangga Batu (39.62%) and Jasin (45.40%) parliamentary areas**. Yet in the 2022 GE15 (one year later), **PN won both federal seats** (Tangga Batu: Bakri 40.65%; Jasin: Zulkifli 35.95%). This one-year swing from BN-led to PN-won is the local manifestation of the 2022 "Green Wave" — the PAS/PN surge that delivered BN's federal wipeout in Malacca (0 of 6 seats). Both Bakri (PIR-15) and Zulkifli (PIR-12) are products of this swing.

### PAS's 2021 zero — context for Zulkifli's mobilisation (PIR-12)
- PAS won **0 state seats in 2021** (6.96% vote) — yet by 2022 it held 2 Malacca federal seats (Zulkifli, Bakri) and by 2026, 1 state seat (Jailani/Rembia, via realignment). PAS's Malacca growth from 0 (2021) to 2 federal + 1 state is the grassroots-mobilisation story PIR-12 seeks to characterise — but it was achieved primarily through the PN umbrella and the 2022 Green Wave rather than through standalone PAS organisational mass. The "commissioner" mobilisation role (if Zulkifli holds it) operates within this PN-assisted growth pattern.

### PKR's 2021 Malacca wipeout — context for Shamsul (PIR-14)
- PKR won 0 state seats in 2021 (-3). Shamsul himself contested Paya Rumput (state) in 2021 and LOST (36.03%, -629). This PKR weakness is the backdrop to Shamsul's 2022 gamble (abandoning Hang Tuah Jaya for Bagan Datuk) and to PKR's near-zero Malacca state base — now being rebuilt by Adam Adli (PIR-11).
