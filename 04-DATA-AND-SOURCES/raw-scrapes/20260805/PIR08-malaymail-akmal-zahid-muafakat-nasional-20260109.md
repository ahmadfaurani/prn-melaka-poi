## Context & Background — THE CRITICAL INTELLIGENCE

- Speculation intensified after Akmal hinted he might **"step aside"** following Umno president **Datuk Seri Ahmad Zahid Hamidi's** rejection of his call for Umno to **leave the federal government**.
- Zahid also dismissed Akmal's proposal to **revive cooperation with PAS** under the **Muafakat Nasional** charter.
- Akmal had tabled the Muafakat Nasional proposal during a **party political bureau meeting on Monday** as a matter of principle.
- A *Malaysiakini* report cited **unnamed sources** claiming Akmal informed Youth leaders via WhatsApp from Shanghai that he would relinquish all positions upon returning.
