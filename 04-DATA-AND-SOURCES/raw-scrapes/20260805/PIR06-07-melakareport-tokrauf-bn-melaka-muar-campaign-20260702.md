## Key Details

- **Location:** Muar, Johor
- **Key Figure:** Datuk Seri Utama Ab Rauf Yusoh ("Tok Rauf") — Pengerusi Badan Perhubungan UMNO Melaka
- **BN Candidate Supported:** Dato' Ashari Md. Sharif (N15 DUN Maharani)
- **Event:** Sembang Santai Bersama PDM Parit Unas @ Warong Atok

## Purpose of Visit

Tok Rauf led the BN Melaka machinery to **strengthen campaign efforts** for the BN candidate in N15 DUN Maharani. Visit demonstrates the party's ongoing commitment to assist local machinery in engaging voters and reinforcing readiness ahead of polling day.

## Core Messages from Tok Rauf

1. **Grassroots Strength**
   - BN's true strength begins at the grassroots level through ground engagement
   - Directly listening to the people's concerns ("denyut nadi rakyat") remains fundamental
   - Close community ties have long been the foundation of BN's struggle

2. **Social Media Strategy**
   - Urged all machinery to master social media as an effective communication medium
   - Strategic social media use complements ground operations

3. **BN's Identity & Culture**
   - BN's culture is to always stay close to the people and serve tirelessly
   - BN is not merely a political party but a platform of struggle prioritizing the people's interests
   - BN carries agendas of development, progress, and well-being for all

## Key Quote
