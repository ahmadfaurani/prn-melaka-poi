### Overview
- **Malay name:** Tentera Laut Diraja Malaysia (TLDM); founded 27 April 1934; ~18,000 personnel.
- **HQ:** **RMN Lumut Naval Base, Lumut, Perak** (NOT Malacca)
- **Motto:** Sedia Berkorban ("Ready to Sacrifice")
- **Patron:** HM The King of Malaysia (Yang di-Pertuan Agong)
- **Captain-in-Chief:** HRH Sultan Sharafuddin Idris Shah of Selangor
- **Chief of Navy:** Admiral Tan Sri Zulhelmy Ithnain (since 1 August 2024)
- **Deputy Chief of Navy:** Vice Admiral Datuk Badarudin Taha

### Area of Operation
- 603,210 km² covering coastal areas and Exclusive Economic Zones (EEZ).
- Controls key **Sea Lines of Communications (SLOC): Straits of Malacca, Straits of Singapore.**
