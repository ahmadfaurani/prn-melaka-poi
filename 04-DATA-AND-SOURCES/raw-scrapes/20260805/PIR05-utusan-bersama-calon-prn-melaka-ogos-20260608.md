## Full Content

### Key Announcement

- **Parti Bersama Malaysia (Bersama)** expects to announce its candidates and seats for the Melaka State Election (PRN) as early as **August 2026**
- Party president: **Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli** (former Economy Minister)
- Announcement made after the second series of **Jelajah Kancil** tour at a hotel in Ayer Keroh

### Timeline & Context

- Current focus is on PRN Johor and Negeri Sembilan (both DUNs dissolved)
- Melaka PRN expected to follow after Johor and Negeri Sembilan elections
- Party needs **~1 more month** to assess voter reception before finalizing seat count
- Target: Clear picture of seats and areas by **early August**

### Key Quotes

> "Menjelang awal Ogos, kita sepatutnya sudah mempunyai gambaran yang lebih jelas mengenai jumlah kerusi dan kawasan yang akan ditandingi di Melaka."

> "Kelebihan Bersama sebagai parti pelbagai kaum yang memilih untuk bergerak secara solo membolehkannya bertanding di mana-mana kawasan tanpa terikat kepada kerusi tradisi mana-mana parti."

### Strategic Positioning

- Bersama operates as a **multiracial party moving solo** (not in coalition)
- Advantage: Can contest any seat without being tied to traditional seats of other parties
- Party aims to offer a **new political alternative** to multiracial voters in Melaka
- Support momentum expected to increase as election approaches
