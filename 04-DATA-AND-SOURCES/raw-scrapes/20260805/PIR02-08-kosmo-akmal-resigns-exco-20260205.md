## Constitutional Context

- Exco positions are **appointed by Yang di-Pertua Negeri** according to the State Constitution.
- Therefore, resignation must be **officially accepted** by the Governor.
- The Chief Minister's statement that Dr. Akmal "is still Exco" reflects this procedural reality, not political maneuvering.
