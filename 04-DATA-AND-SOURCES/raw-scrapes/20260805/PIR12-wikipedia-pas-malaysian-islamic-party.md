## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-04)

### PAS organisational structure & Malacca footprint (PIR-12)
- PAS is a **mass-membership organisation (1,005,700 members, 2022)** with four formal wings (Ulamak, Muslimat, Pemuda, DHPP non-Muslim) plus Siswa. Its grassroots mobilisation model is **cleric-led (Dewan Ulamak) + youth (Dewan Pemuda)**, with Harakah as the party newspaper. This is the organisational template Zulkifli Ismail would deploy as PAS Melaka commissioner (the commissioner role itself remains UNCONFIRMED — see Jasin scrape).
- **PAS's Malacca footprint is SMALL but concentrated:** 2 federal MPs (Zulkifli/Jasin, Bakri/Tangga Batu — both confirmed this cycle), 1 state ADUN (Jailani Khamis/Rembia), out of 43 MPs and 146 ADUNs nationally. Malacca is NOT in PAS's core belt (Kelantan/Terengganu/Kedah/Perlis) — it is a peripheral, contested state where PAS relies on the PN umbrella and the informal BN-PAS (Muafakat) dynamic rather than its own organisational mass.
- **State commissioners** are NOT named on the national PAS Wikipedia page (this is a national-level article). The "PAS Melaka commissioner" title for Zulkifli Ismail remains an open verification item requiring Malay-language sources (harakahdaily.net, Sinar Harian, PAS Melaka social media) — the configured web_search backend cannot retrieve these.

### PAS-Bersatu coordination (PIR-12)
- PAS and BERSATU are both in PN (PAS since 2020). Nationally PAS (43 MPs) vastly outweighs BERSATU (19 MPs). The page notes PAS's 2025-2026 "Ikatan Prihatin Rakyat" affiliation — a secondary alliance signal. Cross-ref CJ-MLK-03: PAS cut political cooperation with BERSATU on 8-9 June 2026 (both remain in PN structurally). In Malacca, this manifests as PAS holding 2 of PN's 3 federal seats and 1 of PN's 3 state seats, with BERSATU marginalised to 1 federal (Mas Ermieyati, status uncertain) and 1 state (Sungai Udang).
