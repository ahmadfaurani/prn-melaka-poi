## Full Content

### Overview
- **Date:** 20 November 2021 | **15th Malacca state election**
- Elect 28 members to Malacca State Legislative Assembly
- Previous assembly dissolved 4 October 2021
- Registered voters: 495,195 | Turnout: 65.85% | Seats needed for majority: 15

### Key Context
- **Snap election** triggered when four MLAs withdrew support from CM Sulaiman Md Ali (4 Oct 2021)
- First state election in Malay Peninsula since fall of PH federal govt (2020)
- First election following Ismail Sabri's appointment as PM (21 Aug 2021)
- **★ Unique feature: Two government coalition partners (BN and PN) competed against each other** — previously only seen in East Malaysia
- Malacca = 4th Malaysian state not holding simultaneous state/federal elections

### Political Crisis Trigger
Four assemblymen declared loss of confidence in CM Sulaiman Md Ali:
1. **Idris Haron** (Sungai Udang MLA, former CM) — BN/UMNO (membership auto-dropped)
2. **Nor Azman Hassan** (Pantai Kundor MLA) — BN/UMNO (membership auto-dropped)
3. **Norhizam Hassan Baktee** (Pengkalan Batu MLA, IND EXCO member)
4. **Noor Effandi Ahmad** (Telok Mas MLA, EXCO member) — PN/BERSATU (membership terminated)

## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-03)

1. **Baseline seat map for next PRN:** BN 21 / PH 5 / PN 2 (of 28). This is the starting position. The CJ-MLK-01 sidebar signal "Melaka BN likely to retain most incumbents" (Star, ~25 Jul 2026) reads against this 21-seat base. Ab Rauf Yusoh (current CM) succeeded Sulaiman Md Ali.

2. **BN-PN competition precedent (CRITICAL for PIR-05):** In 2021, BN and PN — both part of the federal Ismail Sabri government — **fought each other** in Malacca. This is the historical template for the next PRN: BN (Ab Rauf's vehicle) and PN (Mas Ermieyati's vehicle) are structurally competitors in Melaka, not allies, despite both being Malay-Muslim right-leaning. The 8 June 2026 PAS-BERSATU break intensifies this competition.

3. **Mas Ermieyati's Masjid Tanah base:** BN won the Masjid Tanah parliamentary constituency with 54.65% in 2021 — this is Mas Ermieyati's federal seat (MP Masjid Tanah since 2013). At state level, she lost Tanjung Bidara (a Masjid Tanah-component state seat) to Ab Rauf Yusoh in 2021, when she was PN's CM candidate. So the Ab Rauf–Mas Ermieyati rivalry is *direct and constituency-level* — central to PIR-09.

4. **Adly Zahari / AMANAH baseline:** AMANAH held only 1 state seat (Durian Tunggal, lost to BN in 2021) and entered 2021 with 2. Adly Zahari is the sole AMANAH MLA in Melaka (Bukit Katil per CJ-MLK-02). PH's Malacca presence is concentrated in DAP's urban seats (Kota Melaka, Hang Tuah Jaya = Adam Adli's federal seat). AMANAH/PKR are electorally weak in Melaka state — relevant to PIR-10 (Adly's coalition positioning has a thin electoral base).

5. **PKR wiped out (0 seats) in 2021:** PKR lost all contested state seats. Adam Adli (PKR, MP Hang Tuah Jaya) inherits this weak PKR state base — but Hang Tuah Jaya was a PH-won parliamentary constituency (39%). The PKR Melaka chairmanship he now holds (acting, Dec 2025) is a rebuild task on a near-zero base.

6. **PH won Hang Tuah Jaya & Kota Melaka federally** — these are the two non-BN federal seats. Adam Adli (HTJ) and Kota Melaka (DAP) are PH's federal foothold.
