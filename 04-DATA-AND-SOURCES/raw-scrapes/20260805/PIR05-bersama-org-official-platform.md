## Full Content

**BERSAMA** — *Barisan Rakyat, Suara Malaysia* (People's Front, Voice of Malaysia)

### The Kancil Philosophy

Malaysia is a small country with big global potential. The organization draws inspiration from the **_kancil_** (mouse deer), symbolizing the need to rely on smarts and wits to navigate uncertainty.

**Key principles:**
- **Brain prevails over brawn** — The tale of *Sang Kancil dan Buaya* teaches that smarts paired with bravery can overcome disadvantage
- **Clever, Brave, Agile** — The *kancil* represents the average Malaysian who can erode the power of the *buaya* (crocodiles) who have long held sway over society

### Malaysia is Built Together

- Today's Malaysia was built on **sacrifice, hard work, and collaboration of diverse people**
- Current economic and social challenges demand re-embracing **common purpose and common good**

### Bersama Agenda: 12 Key Policies

Programmes must be: Ambitious, Increase social mobility, Ensure social justice, Be sustainable

1. **National Social Security System** — monthly government assistance based on household income/age/dependents, automatic, no pre-registration; consolidate welfare agencies under single Ministry of Social Security
2. **Limiting Foreign Workers to 5% of National Workforce** — national worker database, incentivize local talent, higher-skill jobs
3. **Universal Childcare** — free preschools ages 4-5, 10-year infrastructure rollout
4. **Eliminating Rent-Seeking & Elevating SMEs** — Anti-'Ali Baba' Act; SME targets ≥50% GDP, 60% jobs, 30% exports
5. **Dual-Stream Higher Education Framework** — diploma accreditation pathway, university autonomy
6. **1:300 Doctor-to-Patient Ratio by 2030** (current 1:412)
7. **Upper Quartile PISA Ranking** (Malaysia 56th of 81, May 2026)
8. **Safeguarding Institutional Independence & Decentralizing Power** — transfer powers from PM to Parliament
9. **≥5% Annual GDP Growth & Fair Wage** — real wage growth >5%, high-complexity economic model
10. **Malaysia as Southeast Asia's 'Battery'** — NETR, rooftop residential solar
11. **Modern Agriculture as Major National Industry** — greenhouses, food security hubs (Kelantan, Terengganu, Sarawak)
12. **Next-Generation Housing** — IBS manufacturing, home prices ≤4× annual household income
