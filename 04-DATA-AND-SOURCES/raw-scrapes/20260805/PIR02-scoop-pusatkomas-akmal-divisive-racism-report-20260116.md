## Key Facts & Context
- **The Incident:** Umno Youth Chief Datuk Dr Akmal Salleh vowed to "fight DAP to the very end" during his speech at the Umno Youth General Assembly
- **Akmal's Status:** Resigned as Melaka state executive councillor (Exco) but retained Umno Youth Chief position
- **Political Context:** Umno is a component party of the ruling Madani government coalition, alongside DAP

## Pusat Komas's Condemnation & Arguments

- **Targeting Minorities:** Condemned statement as "blatant political posturing" casting non-Malay communities as targets, rallying anti-Chinese and minority sentiment under guise of "grassroots" loyalty
- **Contradicts Coalition Governance:** As part of governing coalition, Umno should act as partner committed to "cooperation, consensus building and multiracial governance" rather than positioning fellow coalition parties as enemies to be "fought" indefinitely
- **Track Record of Inflammatory Rhetoric:** Citing Pusat Komas's **2023 and 2024 Racism Reports**, Akmal has history of hateful remarks — previously directing racist insults at a Chinese-DAP parliamentarian and multiple remarks against minority communities

## Key Quotes

> "This statement is a blatant political posturing that inevitably casts Malaysia's non-Malay communities as targets."

> "Akmal is essentially rallying anti-Chinese and other minority sentiment under the guise of 'grassroots' loyalty. Such rhetoric implicitly mobilises ethnic animosities for political gain."

> "This country needs leadership that unites, not divides... Scapegoating any political party or ethnicity is incompatible with democratic values."

> "We call on all Umno Youth to have a refreshed mindset for a world where racism is replaced by a Malaysia for all. Only when a political party can truly stand for all does it deserve the support of all Malaysians."

## Calls to Action
- **To Umno Leadership:** Take responsibility and hold Akmal accountable for "reckless and repeated mischievous rhetoric"
- **To All Parties:** Refrain from divisive rhetoric; engage in constructive dialogue
- **To Umno Youth:** Adopt refreshed mindset challenging "old school politics"
