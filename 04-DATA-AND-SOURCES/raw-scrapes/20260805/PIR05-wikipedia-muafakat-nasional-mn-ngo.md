## Full Content

### Overview
**Muafakat Nasional (MN)** (English: **National Concord**) was a Malaysian political coalition formed between **PAS** and **UMNO** to oppose the then-ruling PH after GE14 (2018). Primary aim: unite the Malay/Muslim community (*Ummah*) for electoral purposes.

> *"Muafakat Rakyat, Penyatuan Ummah"* — "National Concord, The Unification of the Ummah"

### History
**Formation (Sep 2019):** Officialised via the **Piagam Muafakat Nasional** — a **five-point charter** signed by PAS president **Abdul Hadi Awang** and UMNO president **Ahmad Zahid Hamidi** at the *Himpunan Penyatuan Ummah* (Ummah Unity Rally), PWTC, KL, 14 Sep 2019.

**BERSATU excluded:** July 2020 MN invited BERSATU; Muhyiddin reaffirmed 15 Aug 2020. But BERSATU simultaneously formed rival coalition PN. 30 Jan 2022 — Najib Razak disclosed BERSATU was **excluded** from MN because it contested against UMNO in the **2020 Sabah state election**.

**Dissolution (15 Dec 2022):** Annuar Musa declared MN was **no longer a political coalition but an NGO**.
