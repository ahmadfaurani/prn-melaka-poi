## Full Content (Wikipedia extraction — Melaka-relevant sections emphasized)

### Overview
- **Malay name:** Tentera Darat Malaysia; founded 1 March 1933; ~80,000 active + ~50,000 reserve personnel.
- The Malaysian Army does not carry the title 'royal' (diraja); the title is bestowed on selected corps/regiments by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong (Supreme Commander: Sultan Ibrahim).

### Leadership (CURRENT)
- **Supreme Commander:** Yang di-Pertuan Agong, Sultan Ibrahim
- **Chief of Army:** General Dato' Azhan Md Othman (since **Jan 1, 2026**) ← current
- **Deputy Chief of Army:** Lt. Gen. Dato' Tengku Muhammad Fauzi Tengku Ibrahim
- **Commander, Western Field Command:** Lt. Gen. Datuk Hj Malek Razak bin Sulaiman
- **Commander, Eastern Field Command:** Lt. Gen. Datuk Mohd Sofi bin Md Lepi

### Field Commands (5 Divisions under two field army HQs)
- **Western Field Army HQ:** 2nd, 3rd, 4th Divisions (Malay Peninsula)
- **Eastern Field Army HQ:** 1st, 5th Divisions (Malaysian Borneo)
