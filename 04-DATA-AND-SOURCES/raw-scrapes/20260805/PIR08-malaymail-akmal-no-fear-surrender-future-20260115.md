## Key Context

- **Event:** Umno General Assembly, January 14–17, 2026, WTCKL
- **Central Figure:** Umno Youth chief **Datuk Dr Muhamad Akmal Saleh**, who hinted at resigning from his posts — including Umno Youth chief and Melaka Exco
- **Expected Announcement:** Dr Akmal's decision anticipated during the Umno Youth assembly

## Background: What Led to the Resignation Hint

1. **Muafakat Nasional Proposal:** Dr Akmal proposed reviving cooperation with PAS (Muafakat Nasional)
2. **Unity Government Withdrawal Call:** He called for Umno to exit the unity government and become Opposition — **firmly rejected by Umno president Datuk Seri Ahmad Zahid Hamidi**
3. **Negative Feedback:** After pushback from party leadership, Dr Akmal hinted at stepping down
4. **Meetings Held:** Dr Akmal met with:
   - Ahmad Zahid (Umno president)
   - Datuk Seri Ab Rauf Yusoh (Melaka Chief Minister)
5. **Formal Motions:** At a recent Umno Youth special convention, Dr Akmal tabled two motions:
   - Resume cooperation with PAS
   - Exit the unity government to become Opposition

## "No Fear, No Surrender" Origin

> Coined during Dr Akmal's public spat with Seputeh MP Teresa Kok in **September 2024** over halal certification remarks, which culminated in a **RM25 million legal demand** against him.

## Grassroots Reactions (Sideline Interviews)

### Razif Abd Rahman — Umno Youth Committee Member, Kota Tinggi
- Resignation decision is personal, not collective
- "At grassroots, there are those who agree and those who do not" (re: Muafakat Nasional revival)
- Questioned consistency: "From grassroots perspective, what he said before does not align with what he's doing now. Why the sudden change?"
- "For the sake of the party, he should stay. Finish your term, at least until after the next general election."

### Mohd Noor Fairuss — Umno Youth Chief, Tanjung Piai Division
- Dr Akmal's outspoken style is a necessity, not a liability
- "He is the voice of the Malays — especially the youth and the grassroots."
- No urgency for resignation — no obvious replacement: "Who else can speak up like him?"

### Dzaini — Jerantut Delegate
- Described Dr Akmal as a political fighter
- Compared his relationship with party president to a son and father

## Key Concerns Raised by Grassroots

| Concern | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | Resignation seen as ill-timed for a party struggling to reclaim Malay support |
| **Consistency** | "No fear, no surrender" slogan contradicted by stepping aside |
| **Leadership Continuity** | No obvious replacement identified; next general election looming |
| **Term Completion** | Calls for Akmal to finish his term at least until after next GE |
