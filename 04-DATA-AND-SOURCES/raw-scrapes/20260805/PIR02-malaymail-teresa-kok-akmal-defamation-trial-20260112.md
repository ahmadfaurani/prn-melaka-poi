## Key Facts

- **Plaintiff:** Teresa Kok, Seputeh MP
- **Defendant:** Datuk Dr Muhamad Akmal Saleh, Umno Youth chief, Merlimau Assemblyman, Melaka Exco (Rural Development, Agriculture and Food Security)
- **Suit filed:** 25 October 2024
- **Mediation venue:** Melaka Court Mediation Centre, before Deputy Registrar Suzana Mokhtar
- **Mediation outcome:** Failed — no settlement after ~1 hour
- **Trial dates:** 12 and 13 February, Melaka High Court

## Key Quote

> "The trial has been fixed for February 12 and 13 at the Melaka High Court. We will therefore meet him (Dr Muhamad Akmal) in court on the scheduled dates." — Datuk Sankara Nair, lawyer for Kok

## Allegations

Kok alleges Dr Muhamad Akmal made **insulting and derogatory statements** portraying her as:
- Lacking knowledge, particularly on matters related to **halal certification**
- Opposing government policies on Islamic practices

The defamatory remarks were allegedly made in a **video posted on TikTok on 7 September 2024**.

## Damages Sought

Kok seeking: General damages, Aggravated damages, Exemplary damages, an **injunction** to restrain Akmal from further defamatory statements, and any other relief deemed just.
