### Early Life & Activism
- Student activist at UPSI. **9 Jan 2013:** suspended 3 semesters for allegedly tarnishing the university's name / disturbing public peace.
- **Sedition case:** 13 May 2013 statements at Selangor Chinese Assembly Hall forum; arrested 18 May 2013; charged under Section 4(1) Sedition Act + Section 125b Penal Code; remanded 5 days; 2014 sentenced to 12 years by Sessions Court; reduced to RM5,000 fine on appeal; **22 Feb 2018: fully acquitted** (conviction "unsafe due to misdirections in law").
