## Key Statements

> "Anyone is welcome to come and negotiate, but negotiations do not necessarily end with an agreement because everything depends on each party's respective strengths."

> "So, we are not bound by any arrangement that has yet to be formally established as a final coalition, but I am open to everyone who wishes to negotiate with me." — **Datuk Seri Ab Rauf Yusoh**, Melaka Chief Minister & BN Chairman

## Summary

- **Melaka BN** open to cooperating with **any political party** under a **"BN Plus" formula** for the upcoming state election
- Ab Rauf (also Melaka Umno liaison committee chairman) emphasized negotiations welcome but don't guarantee formal agreements
- Each party's respective strengths will determine outcomes
- Statement made after the **Jelajah Ketua Menteri Sayang Rakyat (MeSRa) programme** at the **Telok Mas Japerun Complex**

## Context: Negeri Sembilan Precedent

- **BN chairman Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Zahid Hamidi** stated that successful **BN-PN cooperation** in forming the Negeri Sembilan state government would spur winning momentum for Melaka's election
- **NS election results:** BN 18 seats, PH 11 seats, PN 7 seats → BN and PN formed state government together
- New NS Menteri Besar: **Datuk Ismail Lasim** (Juasseh), 6,907 votes (4,091 majority)

## Key Takeaway

Melaka BN adopting an open-door negotiation strategy for the upcoming state election, taking inspiration from successful BN-PN coalition in NS, while maintaining flexibility based on each party's electoral strength.
