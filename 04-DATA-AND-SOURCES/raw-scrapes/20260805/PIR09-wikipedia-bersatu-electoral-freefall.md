### History
- **2016:** Founded by former UMNO members opposing Najib (1MDB fallout); Mahathir joined PH
- **2018 GE:** BERSATU won 13 seats; Mahathir = 7th PM (age 93); mass UMNO defections
- **2020:** PH collapse; Mahathir resigned; BERSATU withdrew; Muhyiddin = 8th PM (PN)
- **May 2020:** Mahathir, Mukhriz, Syed Saddiq, Maszlee, Amiruddin Hamzah expelled (sitting with opposition)
- **Aug 2021:** UMNO's Zahid withdrew support; Muhyiddin resigned 16 Aug; Ismail Sabri (BN) = 9th PM
- **2022 GE:** BERSATU won **35 seats** (14.16%); hung parliament; Zahid formed govt with PH; Muhyiddin rejected King's unity offer → opposition
- **2023 state elections:** Massive gains, nearly took Selangor
- **Post-2023 struggles:** Intraparty conflict (pro-Muhyiddin vs pro-Hamzah factions)
- **Lost 4 consecutive by-elections:** Pulai, Kuala Kubu Baharu, Nenggiri, Mahkota
- **Nov 2025:** Wiped out in Sabah state election
- **★ 2026:** Wiped out in Johor and Negeri Sembilan state elections

2. **Women's Chief = Nolee Ashilin Mohammed Radzi** (also PN Women's Chief). Note: CJ-MLK-02 recorded Mas Ermieyati as "Ketua Srikandi BERSATU" (the Srikandi/women's youth-affiliate wing chief) since Oct 2024 — a *distinct* post from the main Women's Chief (Nolee Ashilin). Both are BERSATU women's-wing leadership nodes; Mas Ermieyati's is the Srikandi (younger women) affiliate.

3. **BERSATU electoral freefall confirmed (PIR-09 core):** Wiped out in Sabah (Nov 2025), Johor (2026), NS (2026); lost 4 consecutive by-elections. "Unable to establish a clear identity" between UMNO and PAS. This is the structural context for Mas Ermieyati's PN strategy: her party cannot win on its own, and PAS has cut cooperation (8-9 June 2026). The viable path for a BERSATU figure is now either (a) accommodation within a PAS-led PN ticket, or (b) defection — the reported (unverified) membership suspension (CJ-MLK-02) hints at factional turbulence.

4. **Historical Malacca partnership ended:** BERSATU "previously partnered" Malacca (state govt) — this was the 2020-21 period when PN was in the state government (1 PN seat pre-2021 election). That ended with the 2021 election (BN won 21; PN became opposition with 2). The Ab Rauf (BN) vs Mas Ermieyati (PN/BERSATU) rivalry crystallised here.

5. **Ikatan Prihatin Rakyat (IPR) affiliation since 2025:** A second national affiliation beyond PN — worth monitoring; may indicate BERSATU hedging or a parallel vehicle.
