## Education & Early Activism
- Studied education at UPSI
- **9 January 2012:** Suspended 3 semesters (until Sep 2013) for "tarnishing the university's name" and "disturbing public safety and order"
- Upon return, suspended again without trial due to ongoing Sedition Act prosecution
- Resumed studies at UPSI after **6 years** of suspension, following acquittal February 2018

## Political Career
- **16 September 2021:** Officially joined PKR alongside ~20 young activists/student leaders
- **2022 PKR Party Elections:** Contested AMK Chief with team "Seangkatan"

## Legal Issues

### Sedition Charges (2013)
- 13 May 2013: Detained for statements at Dewan Perhimpunan Cina Selangor forum
- 18 May 2013: Arrested; charged under Section 4(1) Sedition Act + Section 125b Penal Code
- 2014: Sentenced 12 years imprisonment (Sessions Court); reduced to RM5,000 fine on appeal (High Court)
- **22 February 2018:** Fully acquitted of sedition charges

### Illegal Assembly Charges (2015)
- 28 February 2015: Charged under Section 4(2)(c) Peaceful Assembly Act 2012
- 28 November 2017: Discharged and acquitted (Magistrate's Court)
- 31 November 2017: High Court upheld acquittal (Judge Mohd Nazlan Mohd Ghazali)
