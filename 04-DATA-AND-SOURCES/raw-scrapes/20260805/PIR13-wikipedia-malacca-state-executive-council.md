## Full Content

### Overview
The Malacca State Executive Council (EXCO) is the executive authority of the Government of Malacca. Structurally similar to the Cabinet of Malaysia but smaller.

- **Head of State:** Mohd Ali Rustam (Governor/Yang di-Pertua Negeri)
- **Head of Government:** Ab Rauf Yusoh (Chief Minister, BN–UMNO)
- **Total Members:** 11
- **Date Formed:** 31 March 2023
- **Coalition Government (as formed):** BN + PH (26/28 seats): BN 10 (UMNO 8, MCA 1, MIC 1) + PH 1 (DAP 1)
- **Opposition:** PN–BERSATU (2 seats); **Opposition Leader:** Mohd Yadzil Yaakub (PN–BERSATU)
- Unlike Sabah/Sarawak, the Council has **no ministries** — operates through **committees**; each EXCO chairs a committee.

- Mohd Noor Helmy is a **first-term ADUN** (since the 2021 state election) in a **marginal seat** — Duyong (N21), won by only 200 votes (1.65%) (see Duyong scrape). He is a deputy, not a full EXCO. This is a Malay-seat consolidation within BN, consistent with the broader UMNO reassertion in Malacca. As a UMNO figure in a former-MCA seat, his succession claim is strengthened within UMNO's Malay core but may carry MCA friction.
