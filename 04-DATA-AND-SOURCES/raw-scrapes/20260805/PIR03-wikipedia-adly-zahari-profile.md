## Full Content (Consolidated EN + BM Wikipedia extraction)

### Identity
- **Full Name:** Adly bin Zahari (Jawi: عدلي بن ظهري)
- **Born:** 15 February 1971 (age 55), Kampung Pulau, Durian Tunggal, Malacca, Malaysia
- **Spouse:** Dalilah Awaluddin; **Children:** 7
- **Education:** BEng (Electrical Engineering, Hons), Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)
- **Profession:** Electrical Engineer; Politician
- **Party:** AMANAH (since 2015); formerly PAS (until 2015)
- **Coalition:** Pakatan Harapan (PH) (since 2015); formerly Pakatan Rakyat (2008–2015)

### Current Positions (as of collection date)
- **Deputy Minister of Defence (Timbalan Menteri Pertahanan)** — since 10 December 2022. Appointed by PM Anwar Ibrahim. Ministers served under: Mohamad Hasan (2022–2023), **Mohamed Khaled Nordin (since 12 Dec 2023)**. Preceded by Ikmal Hisham Abdul Aziz.
- **Member of Parliament (MP) for Alor Gajah (P135)** — since 19 November 2022. Won by majority of **890 votes** (28,178 / 38.60%) vs Shahril Sufian Hamdan (UMNO, 27,288/37.38%) and Mohd Redzuan Md Yusof (BERSATU, 17,211/23.58%).
- **MLA for Bukit Katil (N17), Malacca State Legislative Assembly** — since 9 May 2018. **Sole AMANAH MLA in Malacca.** 2018 majority 3,159; 2021 snap-election majority 1,057.
- **Vice President of AMANAH** — since 5 September 2021.
- **State Chairman of PH – Malacca** — since 30 August 2017.
- **State Chairman of PH – Kelantan** — since 5 November 2024 (DUAL state chairmanship — Melaka AND Kelantan).
- **Treasurer of Pakatan Harapan** (coalition-level).
- Former Treasurer-General of AMANAH (prior to VP promotion).

### Previous Positions
- **11th Chief Minister of Malacca** (11 May 2018 – 9 March 2020): first-ever transition of power in Malacca; PH won 15 of 28 state seats. **Rejected a Datukship offer** during tenure. Preceded by Idris Haron; succeeded by Sulaiman Md Ali.
- **State Leader of the Opposition (Malacca)** (24 April 2020 – 13 December 2022): relinquished after PH became strategic partner to CM Sulaiman Md Ali's government. Succeeded by Mohd Yadzil Yaakub.

### Career Signal — Failed Promotion Bid (2023)
- **Speculated** to be promoted to Minister of Domestic Trade and Costs of Living after Salahuddin Ayub's death (July 2023). However, **Armizan Mohd Ali (GRS)** was appointed instead; Adly remained as Deputy Minister of Defence. *(Note: this is a documented non-advancement event — relevant to ministerial-prospect analysis.)*
