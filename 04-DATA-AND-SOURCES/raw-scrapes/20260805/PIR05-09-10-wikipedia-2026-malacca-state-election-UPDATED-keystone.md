## Full Content (Updated)

### Overview
The **2026 Malacca state election** will elect members of the **16th Malacca State Legislative Assembly**. It must be held **on or before November 2026**, per clause 19(4) of the Constitution of Malacca, unless dissolved earlier. The assembly is likely to be dissolved within three months, with a possible election by **September 2026**.

### Key Electoral Facts
- **Total seats:** 28 (all contested)
- **Majority needed:** 15 seats
- **Voting system:** First-past-the-post, single-member constituencies
- **Voting age:** 18 (automatic voter registration; no compulsory voting)
- **Term length:** 5 years

### Background & Political Timeline

**2023:** PH Joins BN-Led State Government — BN invited PH to join the state government to mirror the federal-level unity government coalition.

**2026: PH Withdrawal from State Government**
- **14 July 2026:** BN-led government moved to amend the state Constitution to allow nomination/appointment of **7 unelected assembly members**.
- Amendment **passed 23–5** (all 21 BN + 2 PN in support; all 5 PH against).
- **DAP withdrew** from the state government the same day in protest.
- DAP representatives moved to opposition benches on **15 July**; however, state PH chief **Adly Zahari (Amanah)** initially remained with the government side.
- **16 July:** Melaka Pakatan chairman Zahari reaffirmed PH's departure from the state government, agreed by all 3 component parties (PKR, DAP, Amanah), **despite PM Anwar Ibrahim's call** for DAP to defer its departure.

### Preparations & Political Positioning
- **17 May 2026:** CM disclosed the state election could be held within **120 days** of his statement.
- **15 July 2026:** Bersama party de facto leader **Rafizi Ramli** announced Bersama would not contest NS, instead focusing on **Malacca**.
- Seat negotiations among opposition parties expected to be a major factor.
