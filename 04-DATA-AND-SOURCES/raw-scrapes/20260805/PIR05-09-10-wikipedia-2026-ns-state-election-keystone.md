## Analyst Notes (CJ-MLK-03) — KEYSTONE ANALYSIS

### A. The BN-PN Informal Pact is the New Winning Formula (PIR-05)
The NS result (1 Aug 2026) is the single most important coalition-dynamics datum of the cycle. **BN and PN contested as an informal pact and won a two-thirds majority (25/36).** Critically, the *composition* of the winning side reveals the realignment:
- **BN: UMNO 16 + MCA 2 = 18**
- **PN: PAS 4 + WAWASAN 3 = 7** (PAS + the Hamzah-formed WAWASAN)
- **BERSATU contested SOLO and won ZERO seats.**

So the electorally viable Malay-Muslim opposition bloc is now **UMNO + PAS (+ WAWASAN)**, with BERSATU excluded. This is, in substance, a **reactivated Muafakat Nasional (UMNO+PAS)** — exactly the alignment Dr Akmal Saleh has been advocating (CJ-MLK-01 keystone). Zahid rejected Akmal's *formal* proposal, but the NS election shows the *de facto* cooperation is already happening at the state level. This is the central finding for PIR-05.

### B. Tok Mat Led the Winning BN Campaign (cross-ref CJ-MLK-01 Suggestion 3)
The BN side was led by **Mohamad Hasan ("Tok Mat")** — the DPM and the very figure CJ-MLK-01 flagged as needing investigation re: Ab Rauf's federal relationship. Tok Mat personally led the NS campaign that delivered BN 18 seats. This establishes Tok Mat as the active federal UMNO election strategist — highly relevant to how the next Melaka PRN will be run and to Ab Rauf's autonomy question (PIR-06).

### C. PAS–BERSATU Break, Mechanism Clarified (PIR-09)
The NS page clarifies *why* PAS cut ties with BERSATU (9 June 2026): BERSATU assemblymen had withdrawn support for UMNO, which PAS (cooperating with UMNO) saw as a betrayal. The 25 May Muhyiddin letter reveals BERSATU was *itself* plotting with PAS against PH (to remove Aminuddin) — but BERSATU's subsequent move against UMNO caused PAS to sever the BERSATU link. Net: **PAS chose UMNO over BERSATU.** For Mas Ermieyati (BERSATU's Ketua Srikandi, MP Masjid Tanah), this means her party is now the isolated, electorally-sterile component of PN, while PAS — PN's elections director (Sanusi) and chairman (Samsuri) — runs the show.

### D. BERSATU's Electoral Freefall (PIR-09)
Confirmed across the BERSATU page: wiped out in **Sabah (Nov 2025), Johor (2026), and NS (2026)** state elections; lost 4 consecutive by-elections (Pulai, Kuala Kubu Baharu, Nenggiri, Mahkota). "The Malay vote remained mostly split between UMNO and PAS, with Bersatu unable to establish a clear identity." Mas Ermieyati sits atop a party in existential electoral decline — her Masjid Tanah seat is one of BERSATU's last Malacca footholds (1 of Malacca's 6 MP seats held by BERSATU).

### E. PH/Amanah Weakness (PIR-10)
PH lost the government. AMANAH won **0 seats** in NS (DAP 9, PKR 2, AMANAH 0). This mirrors Melaka 2021 (AMANAH 1, PKR 0). AMANAH is the weakest PH component electorally. Adly Zahari (AMANAH VP, sole AMANAH MLA in Melaka) leads a structurally weak party in state elections — his coalition positioning (PIR-10) is in a PH whose only reliable Melaka engine is DAP.

### F. Malacca PRN is Imminent (PIR-05)
**Bersama (Rafizi Ramli's new party) withdrew from NS specifically "to focus on the Malacca election."** This is direct confirmation that the Malacca PRN is the next contested state election and that a new entrant (Bersama) is targeting it. Combined with Kosmo's "Ab Rauf pertahan kemenangan BN" / "BN-PN mampu kuasai Melaka" (2 Aug 2026, per CJ-MLK-01) and the Aug 4 admin reshuffle, the Melaka PRN is activating NOW.

### G. The Unity-Government Strain (cross PIR-05/10)
30 April 2026: Zahid announced UMNO would "remain in unity government with PH" — even as the NS crisis showed UMNO and PH at each other's throats. Anthony Loke's dissolution call (24 May) cited a "trust issue" with UMNO. The federal BN-PH unity marriage is under severe strain, even as the *state-level* BN-PN (UMNO+PAS) pact is winning. DAP's "put government role to vote" (4 Aug) is the latest escalation. The Melaka state government (Ab Rauf's BN, in coalition with PH) sits on this fault line.
