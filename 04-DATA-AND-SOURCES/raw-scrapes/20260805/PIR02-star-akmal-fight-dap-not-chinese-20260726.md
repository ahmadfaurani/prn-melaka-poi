## Key Figure

- **Datuk Dr Muhamad Akmal Saleh** — Umno Youth Chief (Ketua Pemuda UMNO Malaysia)

## Core Message

Akmal Saleh clarified that his political fight is directed at **DAP and Pakatan Harapan**, not against Chinese or Indian communities.

### Key Quote

> "We have given it time and we can work together but we draw our lines – do not touch our religion, race or our rulers. If you do, it doesn't matter if you are a friend or foe, we will fight you till the end."

## Main Points

### 1. Consistent Stance Since Unity Government Formation
- Akmal stated his position has been **consistent since the unity government was formed at the Federal level**.

### 2. Umno Youth Did Not Invite Pakatan to 2023 Assembly
- Umno Youth **never invited Pakatan leaders** to attend the 2023 Umno general assembly.
- Invitations came from **other leaders**, including those from the party's **Wanita and Puteri wings**.

### 3. Conditional Friendship
> "If you want to be friends, we can but do not enter my house yet. If you sincerely want to be friends, then we will do so. But if you are not serious, there is no place for you in my house."

### 4. Challenge to Government on "Racist" Label
- Akmal questioned whether being firm on the following issues is racist:
  - **Special position of the Malays**
  - **Sovereignty of the Malay Rulers**
  - **Sanctity of Islam**

> "If doing so is racist or against the law, I challenge the government to arrest me. I am willing to be arrested if my stance is an offence."
