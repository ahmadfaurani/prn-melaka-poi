## Article 1: PH-BN Melaka Officially "Divorced" (16 Jul 2026)

**Title:** Tidak lagi bersama, PH-BN Melaka sah 'bercerai'
**Date:** 16/07/2026, 21:42 MYT | **Author:** Shuhada Abdul Kadir

### Ringkasan Utama
Pakatan Harapan (PH) Melaka secara rasmi mengesahkan pengeluaran 5 ADUN daripada pentadbiran Kerajaan Negeri, menandakan berakhirnya kerjasama PH-BN di Melaka. Keputusan ini susulan penolakan PH terhadap pindaan Enakmen Perlembagaan Negeri Melaka bagi mewujudkan jawatan ADUN Lantikan.

### Pemimpin yang Menandatangani Kenyataan Bersama
| Nama | Jawatan |
|------|---------|
| **Adly Zahari** | **Pengerusi PH Melaka** |
| **Adam Adli Abdul Halim** | **Pemangku Pengerusi Majlis Pimpinan PKR Melaka** |
| Khoo Poay Tiong | Pengerusi DAP Melaka |
| Datuk Ashraf Mukhlis Minghat | Pengerusi AMANAH Melaka |

### ADUN yang Menarik Diri
- **4 ADUN DAP** dan **1 ADUN Amanah** melepaskan semua jawatan:
  - Jawatan Exco
  - Timbalan Exco
  - Timbalan Speaker
  - Lantikan berkaitan pentadbiran negeri

### Pelepasan Jawatan Lain
- Semua Ahli Majlis Pihak Berkuasa Tempatan (PBT) dan Jawatankuasa Pembangunan dan Keselamatan Kampung (JPKK) daripada gabungan PH akan melepaskan jawatan
- **Berkuat kuasa: 31 Julai 2026**

### Petikan Penting
> "Penarikan diri daripada Kerajaan Negeri ini adalah selaras dengan prinsip dan amalan demokrasi bahawa apabila seseorang Exco atau ADUN dari Kerajaan Negeri tidak lagi menyokong sesuatu usul oleh Kerajaan Negeri di dalam Dewan Undangan Negeri, adalah tidak layak lagi bagi Exco atau ADUN tersebut menerima keistimewaan dan kemudahan Kerajaan Negeri."
> "Lima ADUN PH juga akan terus menumpukan perhatian dan melipat gandakan usaha serta khidmat buat rakyat negeri Melaka tanpa sebarang diskriminasi politik sehingga tamat mandat yang diberikan."

### Latar Belakang
- **Selasa (14 Julai 2026):** PH Melaka mengumumkan menarik sokongan terhadap kerajaan negeri sebagai bantahan terhadap kelulusan Rang Undang-Undang Enakmen Perlembagaan Negeri Melaka (Pindaan) 2026
- Pindaan tersebut memperkenalkan pelantikan sehingga **7 ADUN Lantikan** di Melaka
- PH Melaka merakamkan penghargaan kepada Ketua Menteri Datuk Seri Ab Rauf Yusoh, Kerajaan Negeri, dan penjawab awam negeri atas kerjasama sepanjang tempoh pentadbiran bersama

## Article 2: Adam Adli / PKR Melaka Caution (15 Jul 2026)

**Title:** Jangan tergesa-gesa buat keputusan jejas kestabilan negeri - Keadilan Melaka
**Date:** 15/07/2026 | **Agency:** Bernama

### Key Development
DAP Melaka exited the state government immediately after the State Legislative Assembly (DUN) passed a constitutional amendment enabling the appointment of ADUNs via appointment rather than election.

### PKR Melaka's Response (Adam Adli)
**Pemangku Pengerusi Majlis Pimpinan Negeri Keadilan Melaka Adam Adli Abdul Halim** (also Deputy Minister of Higher Education) issued a statement urging:
- All parties NOT to rush decisions that could jeopardize administrative stability, public welfare, state development
- The constitutional amendment issue be resolved through muafakat (consensus) and negotiation

### Key Quotes (Adam Adli)
> "Pada masa sama, cadangan pelantikan ADUN lantikan perlu dinilai secara teliti berasaskan prinsip kebertanggungjawaban, integriti dan semangat demokrasi."
> "Ruang perbincangan perlu terus dibuka supaya setiap keputusan dapat dicapai secara matang, bertanggungjawab dan demi kepentingan rakyat Melaka."

### Important Clarifications
| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| PKR's stance on 5 ADUNs | Takes note of and respects their position |
| Withdrawal decision | NOT collectively agreed at PH Melaka leadership level |
| PKR Melaka's principle | Political/administrative stability is essential for state progress, economic growth, people's welfare |
| PH Chairman Anwar Ibrahim's directive | Differences should be resolved through consensus, reasoned negotiation, prioritizing people's interests |

### Anwar Ibrahim's Intervention
- Requested DAP Melaka to postpone its decision to exit the state administration
- Focus should be on development and public welfare

### DAP Melaka's Position
- Pengerusi DAP Melaka: Khoo Poay Tiong
- Reason for exit: The constitutional amendment bill contradicts democratic principles and electoral processes
- Action: Announced immediate withdrawal from state government

### Core Issue: Enakmen Perlembagaan Negeri (Melaka) (Pindaan) 2026
- Subject: Amendment to allow appointment of ADUNs (rather than election)
- Status: Passed by Dewan Undangan Negeri Melaka
- Contention: DAP views this as anti-democratic; PKR urges careful evaluation

## Analysis Notes (Collector)

This is a MAJOR cross-POI Melaka-nexus event. BOTH Adly Zahari (Pengerusi PH Melaka) and Adam Adli (Pemangku Pengerusi PKR Melaka) are named as signatories of the joint statement formalizing PH's exit from the Melaka state government (16 Jul 2026). This directly addresses:
- PIR-03: Adly's Melaka political leadership role (PH Melaka Chair) — he led PH's position on the constitutional amendment crisis
- PIR-11: Adam Adli's Melaka political role (Acting PKR Melaka Chair) — he cautioned against rushed decisions and signed the divorce statement

The trigger was the Melaka DUN constitutional amendment allowing up to 7 appointed ADUN (passed 14 Jul), which PH opposed as anti-democratic. The divorce takes effect 31 Jul 2026.

Notable nuance: Adam Adli's statement (15 Jul) was MORE conciliatory than the final outcome — he urged consensus and cautioned against rushed decisions, and noted the withdrawal was "NOT collectively agreed at PH Melaka leadership level." Yet he signed the final divorce statement (16 Jul). This shows Adam Adli as a moderating voice within PH Melaka who ultimately aligned with the collective PH position.

Adly's role was more assertive — he defended the RUU rejection and warned 7 appointed ADUN could erode democracy, while initially supporting CM Ab Rauf (15 Jul) before the formal divorce (16 Jul).

This event has implications for PRN Melaka (state election) timing — PH-BN Melaka cooperation is now dissolved, reshaping the electoral landscape.
