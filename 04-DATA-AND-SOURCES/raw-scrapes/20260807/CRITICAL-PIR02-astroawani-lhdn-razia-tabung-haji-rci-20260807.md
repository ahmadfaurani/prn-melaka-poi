## LHDN Raids (Primary Development)

- **LHDN (Inland Revenue Board) raided 3 premises** linked to former top leadership of Lembaga Tabung Haji (TH)
- Raids follow revelations from the **Royal Commission of Inquiry (RCI)** report on TH
- The target was a **former high-ranking leader** of Tabung Haji
- Basis: RCI report findings

## Tabung Haji Board Response
- TH board states it **supports government's firm action** on RCI findings
- TH is **reviewing NGO memorandums** and **continuing implementation of remaining RCI recommendations**

## Daie Madani & NGO Memorandum (Same Day)
- **Daie Madani and several organizations submitted memorandums** supporting TH integrity enhancement
- Emphasis: return to Tabung Haji Act priorities (investment and welfare of pilgrims)
- TH board reviewing the memorandum

## AMK (UMNO Youth) vs PAS on RCI Timing (Politik page, same day)
- **AMK criticizes PAS** for questioning the timing of RCI disclosure rather than debating the report's content
- AMK statement: *"Jika tidak boleh sanggah fakta, jangan main sentimen rakyat"* (If you can't refute facts, don't play with people's sentiments)
- PAS accused of practicing **"mengalih tiang gol"** (shifting goalposts) politics
