## Key Development

After labelling Perikatan Nasional as **"toxic"** with PAS at the helm of the coalition, Bersatu president Muhyiddin Yassin says his party will form a new political alliance following the Negeri Sembilan state election.

## New Coalition Details

- Bersatu will form the new coalition with other parties, including those part of **Ikatan Prihatin Rakyat (IPR)**, such as **Ibrahim Ali's Putra** and **P Ramasamy's Urimai**
- The new coalition will take part in the **next general election**
- Quote: *"After this state election, Bersatu will no longer be alone. We will form a new grand coalition."*

## Muhyiddin's Accusations Against PAS

> "They think that because they're a Muslim party, they never lie. They're always in the right, while we are always in the wrong."

- Muhyiddin accused PAS of leading PN astray from its core struggle and slandering the Bersatu leadership to sideline his party

## Timeline Context

- **Last month (June 2026):** PAS ended its political cooperation with Bersatu
- PAS **refused to help** with Bersatu's campaign in the **July 11 Johor state election** though both contested under PN's banner
- After being **wiped out in the Johor election**, PAS led PN in reaching an electoral pact with BN for the NS polls
- **NS seat split:** BN contests 25/36, PN vies for 11
- **Bersatu contests under its OWN banner** — named 24 candidates (NOT part of PN's 11)

## Bersatu Capacity Claims

- 700,000 members
- 4,000+ branches
- 180 divisions nationwide
