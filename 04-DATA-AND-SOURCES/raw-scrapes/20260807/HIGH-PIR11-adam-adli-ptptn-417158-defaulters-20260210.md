## Full Content

Timbalan Menteri Pendidikan Tinggi **Adam Adli Abdul Halim** disclosed in **Dewan Rakyat** (statement 10 Feb 2026) the following PTPTN figures:

### Key Figures
- **417,158** PTPTN borrowers categorised as defaulters (culus) — still not making repayments
- **RM77.5 billion** total PTPTN loans disbursed to date
- **4.26 million** students have received PTPTN loans
- **0.7%** of total defaulters are barred from traveling abroad
- **13,000** students have benefited from the free-education initiative so far

### Travel Restrictions on Defaulters
- Only **high-income defaulters** meeting specific criteria face travel bans
- Travel restrictions do NOT apply to:
  - Recent graduates who just completed studies
  - Borrowers who currently have no income

> "Hanya peminjam berpendapatan tinggi yang culas dan mengikut beberapa kriteria sahaja akan dikenakan sekatan perjalanan." — Adam Adli

### Free Education Initiative (response to Datuk Seri Dr Ismail Abd Muttalib, PN-Maran)
> "Isu ini bukan sahaja mengenai menghapuskan hutang, tetapi usaha menyediakan pendidikan percuma."
- Government has begun free-education initiative for those in need; 13,000 students enrolled
