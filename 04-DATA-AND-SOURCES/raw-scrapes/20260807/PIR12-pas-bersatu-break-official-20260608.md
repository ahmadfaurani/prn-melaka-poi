## Full Content

PAS officially decided to end political cooperation with BERSATU effective immediately, announced by PAS President Tan Sri Tuan Guru Abdul Hadi Awang (MP for Marang).

The decision was made at the Special Meeting of the PAS Central Working Committee at PAS Headquarters on Monday, 8 June 2026.

### Five Resolutions

1. **Endorsed** the decision of the Majlis Syura Ulamak meeting (2 June 2026) regarding considerations of dasar, hukum, dan maslahah (policy, religious ruling, and public interest) of PAS political cooperation on the principle of ummah unity.

2. **Reviewed** the position and direction of PAS–BERSATU political cooperation based on reports, studies, and current assessments.

3. **Terminated** political cooperation with BERSATU.

4. **Exploring** new political understanding and electoral pacts for ummah unity, in preparation for State Elections (PRN) and 16th General Election (PRU16).

5. **Accepting** intentions of academics, professionals, political leaders, and social activists to join PAS.

### Background & Context

- Relationship deterioration: The relationship between the two main PN pillar parties began cracking since last month.
- PAS grievances: PAS openly expressed disappointment over several BERSATU actions considered to have violated the spirit of the alliance established since PN's formation.
- BERSATU internal crisis: BERSATU's own internal crisis, characterized by unstable leadership structure, further prompted PAS to reassess the maslahah and impact of the political cooperation on the ummah unity agenda.
- Decision basis: The decision was made after considering policy, religious rulings, and public interest, along with comprehensive reports and studies on recent developments involving the ally party.

### Future Direction

Despite cutting ties with BERSATU, PAS emphasized it will:
- Continue exploring new cooperation and political understanding with other parties
- Ensure continuity of the Malay-Islamic unity agenda in the country
- Prepare strategically for upcoming PRN and PRU16
- Welcome academics, professionals, political leaders, and social activists to join PAS
