## Analysis — Ab Rauf's Governance Agenda (PIR-01)

This is the authoritative statement of Ab Rauf's core policy framework as Ketua Menteri:
1. **"Melaka Sayang Rakyat" (MeSRa)** is his flagship governance brand — a welfare-first, people-centric framing
2. **Three-pillar agenda**: economic development, human capital/welfare, governance/integrity — a standard UMNO state-government template aligned with federal BN priorities
3. **Religious affairs + education + state finance** are the operational focus areas — notably religious affairs (MAIM) as first meeting signals Islamic governance priority
4. **Kampung Digital initiative** (18 Apr) is a flagship modernization project positioned as proof of "UMNO capability in administration"
5. The framing is **federally-aligned** — no divergence from BN/UMNO directives visible; standard welfare+governance+integrity rhetoric consistent with Zahid-era UMNO messaging
