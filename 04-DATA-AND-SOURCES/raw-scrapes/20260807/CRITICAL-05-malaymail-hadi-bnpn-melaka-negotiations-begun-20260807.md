## Key Development

PAS president **Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang** confirmed that **preliminary discussions** have begun between **Perikatan Nasional (PN)** and **Barisan Nasional (BN)** on seat allocations for the upcoming **Melaka state election**.

According to New Straits Times (NST), Hadi said the talks were at an **early stage**, with further negotiations expected in the **weeks ahead**.

## Key Quote

> "The Melaka state assembly has not been dissolved, but discussions, particularly on Malay Muslim unity, are ongoing. PN and BN are working to unite and save the Malays."
> — Hadi Awang, after Friday prayers in Marang

## Critical Details

- Talks are at an **early stage** with further negotiations expected in **coming weeks**
- Negotiations will factor in the **last general election outcome**, where **BN failed to win any parliamentary seats in Melaka**
- Hadi's remarks came in response to **BN chairman Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Zahid Hamidi**, who stated BN's intention to **defend 21 of the 28 seats** it currently holds

## Related Development

Hadi **welcomed the inclusion** of two PN assemblymen in the **Negri Sembilan executive council** under Menteri Besar **Datuk Ismail Lasim**.
