## Negeri Sembilan BN-PN Coalition Government (Federal-State Model for Melaka)

### Historic BN-PN Coalition
- **10 ADUN BN-PN dilantik Exco** — First-ever BN-PN coalition in state administration
- **Mentri Besar Datuk Ismail Lasim** leads **three key portfolios** for 2026-2031 term
- **Risk of "dua pusat pengaruh" (two centers of influence)** flagged by Mujibu due to unique BN-PN coalition structure
- New Exco expected to act as bridge between government and citizens, explaining policies to the public

### BN-PN Seat Formula (Reference for Melaka)
- **Datuk Mohd Razlan Rafii** (UMNO MKT member): BN-PN cooperation and seat distribution formula used in Negeri Sembilan can serve as **reference for upcoming elections** to avoid Malay vote split
- This is directly relevant to Melaka PRN — the NS BN-PN seat formula is the template
