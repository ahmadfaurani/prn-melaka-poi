## Full Content (Consolidated from 3 sources)

### Headline Facts

Dewan Undangan Negeri (DUN) Melaka passed the Enakmen Perlembagaan Negeri Melaka (Pindaan) 2026 enabling the appointment of up to 7 appointed ADUNs.

**Vote Result:** 23 ADUN supported vs. 5 PH ADUN rejected (two-thirds majority achieved)

### Three Main Objectives of the Amendment

1. **Strengthen Representation in DUN**
   - Involve qualified individuals in: legislation, economics, education, investment, technology, state development
   - Contribute to policy formulation and debate

2. **Give Space to Underrepresented Groups**
   - Women, youth, Orang Asli, minority communities, professionals, industry sector representatives
   - Those who may not succeed through elections but can contribute

3. **Improve Quality of Debate**
   - Appointed members provide technical and objective views
   - Strengthen check-and-balance function on legislation, policy, and administration

### Key Quotes

**Ab Rauf Yusoh (Chief Minister):**
"Kedua, memberikan ruang kepada kumpulan yang kurang diwakili. Pelantikan ini boleh meningkatkan penyertaan golongan wanita, belia, Orang Asli, komuniti minoriti, profesional, wakil sektor industri yang mungkin tidak berjaya melalui proses pilihan raya, tetapi mampu menyumbang kepada pembangunan negeri."

"Bila-bila boleh (lantik), tetapi kita dah masuk penghujung (tempoh tamat penggal DUN), buat apa kita nak lantik. Perkara ini bukan untuk kepentingan parti yang memerintah, tetapi akan memberikan (manfaat) kepada mana-mana pihak yang mungkin mentadbir negeri ini nanti."

**Dr Mohd Yadzil Yaakub (Opposition Leader):**
"Pembangkang menyokong usul itu kerana melihat perkara tersebut mempunyai nilai kepada pembangunan institusi DUN jika dilaksanakan dengan betul, mekanisme yang telus dan berteraskan kepentingan rakyat."

### Context

- **BN 2021 Manifesto:** The amendment was a BN manifesto commitment in PRN Melaka 2021, under "Teras 1: Kestabilan Politik (Membudayakan Politik Matang)"
- **Not a backdoor:** Ab Rauf emphasized the amendment is not to create a political backdoor but to strengthen DUN as a more inclusive, knowledgeable institution
- **No immediate appointments:** No appointed ADUN will be appointed in the near future because the DUN term is ending
- **DUN term ends:** December 2026
- **Federal precedent:** Dr Mohd Yadzil cited Dewan Negara senator appointments as a precedent for expertise contributing to legislation
