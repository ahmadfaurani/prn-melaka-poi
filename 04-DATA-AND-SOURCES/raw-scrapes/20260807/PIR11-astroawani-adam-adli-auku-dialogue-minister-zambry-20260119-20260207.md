## AUKU = Akta Universiti dan Kolej Universiti (Universities and University Colleges Act 1971)
Malaysian law governing university administration and student conduct. Longstanding criticism for restricting student freedoms. Student movement demands ABOLITION (mansuh); government position = IMPROVEMENT (perbaiki).
