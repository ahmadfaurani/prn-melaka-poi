## Key Quotes

> *"Jawatan dan pangkat hanyalah sementara, tetapi maruah dan prinsip kekal selamanya."*

> *"Hari yang terakhir saya di Pejabat Exco, Kerajaan Negeri Melaka. Moga kita bertemu lagi."*

## Summary

**Datuk Dr. Muhamad Akmal Salleh**, Ketua Pemuda UMNO, officially ended his service as **Exco Kerajaan Negeri Melaka** today.

### Key Details:
- **Announced resignation** during his policy speech at the **Perhimpunan Agung Pemuda UMNO** (UMNO Youth General Assembly), causing excitement among delegates
- Resignation **effective immediately** on 19 January 2026
- Decision follows UMNO's stance to **remain in Kerajaan Perpaduan** (Unity Government)
- Dr. Akmal had previously been **vocal about concerns** regarding cooperation with DAP
- He **accepted the party's decision** despite his earlier reservations

### Stated Reasons:
- **Not** about position or rank
- To **defend grassroots voices** (suara akar umbi)
- To **protect his political dignity** (maruah politik)

---

## Analysis
- Akmal frames resignation as principle over position — "maruah dan prinsip kekal selamanya"
- The "suara akar umbi" (grassroots voice) justification links to PIR-07 (mobilization) — he positions himself as the grassroots' representative sacrificing office for their anti-DAP sentiment
- This is the trigger event for the Jan-Apr Exco resignation arc that culminates in Ab Rauf rejecting the resignation (8 Apr)
