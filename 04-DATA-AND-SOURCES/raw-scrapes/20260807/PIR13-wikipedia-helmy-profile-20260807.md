### Political Career

**State Assembly Candidacy:**
- First entered electoral politics as the BN candidate for Duyong in the 2021 Malacca state election.
- Achieved an electoral upset, defeating incumbent Damian Yeo Shen Li (DAP), who had held the seat since 2013.
- Won by a narrow margin of 200 votes.
- Prior to state election, served as a City Councilor within the Malacca City Council (MBMB).
- Appointed Deputy EXCO Member following a state cabinet reshuffle under Chief Minister Ab Rauf Yusoh.

**Career in UMNO and BN:**
- Former Youth Chief (Ketua Pemuda) of UMNO Kota Melaka.
- Currently a Member of the Executive Committee (EXCO/AJK) for the UMNO Kota Melaka division.

### Views — Illegal Immigration

- Gained significant popularity on TikTok for on-the-ground work and a no-nonsense attitude.
- As of June 2025: 1.2 million followers and 32.4 million likes on TikTok.
- Holds strict views on PATIs (Pendatang Asing Tanpa Izin — illegal immigrants), particularly ethnic Rohingyas residing in Malaysia without proper documentation.
- Has publicly questioned how Rohingyas could qualify for university admission and advocated for immediate deportation of detained foreign nationals.
