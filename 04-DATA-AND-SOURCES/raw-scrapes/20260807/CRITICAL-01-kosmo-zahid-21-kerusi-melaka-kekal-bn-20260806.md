## Full Content

### Key Facts

- **Barisan Nasional (BN)** will not surrender seats it won in the previous Melaka State Election (PRN) to any party in the upcoming state election
- **Ahmad Zahid Hamidi** (BN Chairman & Deputy Prime Minister) stated this position is final to defend seats based on the people's mandate
- **Melaka DUN breakdown:** 28 total seats; BN currently holds **21 seats**
- The **Melaka Chief Minister** (also BN state chairman) has committed to defending all 21 seats

### Key Quotes

> "Kita telah menetapkan bahawa kerusi yang kita menang, kita tidak akan pernah berkongsi kerusi itu dengan mana-mana pihak."

> "Daripada 21 kerusi, daripada 28 kerusi di Melaka, maka Ketua Menteri selaku Pengerusi BN Negeri Melaka telah memaklumkan kepada saya bahawa beliau berazam untuk mempertahankan kedudukan seluruh 21 kerusi yang dimenangi itu."

### Important Caveat

- **Seat distribution discussions remain open** — Any negotiations regarding seat division among involved parties can still be discussed in upcoming meetings
- The Melaka Chief Minister showed an **open attitude**; negotiations needed among all parties to reach best understanding

### Context

- Statement made after attending the **Majlis Perhimpunan Bulanan KKDW** (Ministry of Rural Development Monthly Assembly) in Putrajaya
- This signals BN's firm stance on retaining its electoral strongholds while leaving room for coalition negotiations
