## Full Content

Jawatankuasa Kira-Kira Wang Negara (PAC) akan memulakan prosiding terhadap **tiga isu utama** yang dibangkitkan dalam **Laporan Ketua Audit Negara (LKAN) 1/2026**, menurut Pengerusi PAC Datuk Mas Ermieyati Samsudin.

### Three PAC Proceedings (from LKAN 1/2026)

**1. UKM (Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia) — Pengurusan & Tadbir Urus**
- Breaches of university constitution
- Revenue-collection activities without mandate
- Conflicts of interest among public officers
- Failure in building-upgrade project management and dilapidated facilities

**2. Program R&D&C&I (Penyelidikan, Pembangunan, Pengkomersialan & Inovasi)**
- Performance deficit
- Waste and critical governance problems
- Loss of intellectual assets

> "Melalui pelaporan LKAN, ekosistem R&D&C&I ini jelas berhadapan dengan defisit prestasi serta pembaziran dan permasalahan tadbir urus yang kritikal, serta kehilangan aset intelektual." — Mas Ermieyati

**3. Pelaburan Hartanah Berhad (PHB)**
- Company management issues
- Physical-project governance crisis

### Proceeding Details
- Proceeding date: to be announced later
- Witness list: to be announced later

### Background
Proceedings initiated after PAC heard briefing from Auditor-General re two main LKAN 1/2026 reports:
1. Penyata Kewangan Agensi Persekutuan Tahun 2024 (Federal Agencies Financial Statements 2024)
2. Aktiviti Kementerian, Jabatan, Badan-Badan Berkanun Persekutuan & Pengurusan Syarikat Kerajaan Persekutuan

### PAC Commitment (Mas Ermieyati)
> "Menjadi komitmen berterusan PAC terhadap obligasi semak dan imbang bagi memastikan setiap sen wang pembayar cukai diurus dengan integriti, ketelusan dan kecekapan tertinggi."
