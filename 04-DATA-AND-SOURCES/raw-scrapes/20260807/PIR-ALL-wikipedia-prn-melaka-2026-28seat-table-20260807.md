## Key Intelligence

### PAS Current PN Allocation (pre-break, from Wikipedia)
PAS seats: N01, N05, N06, N09, N14, N17, N21, N26, N27 = **9 seats**
BERSATU seats: N02, N03, N04, N07, N08, N11, N12, N13, N15, N18, N23, N25, N28 = **13 seats**
GERAKAN seats: N10, N16, N19, N20, N22 = **5 seats**
WAWASAN seats: N24 = **1 seat**
Total PN: 28 seats

### Post-Break PAS Expansion (FMT, 4 Aug 2026)
PAS announced intention to contest **16 seats** (not 9). This means PAS is expanding by 7 seats beyond its current PN allocation — taking over BERSATU/GERAKAN seats. Including:
- **N02 Tanjung Bidara** (Ab Rauf's seat, currently BERSATU allocation)
- PAS is contesting BN-held seats AND its own PN-held seats

### Critical Discrepancy
The Wikipedia page shows **pre-break** PN allocations (BERSATU in 13 seats). Post-break (8 Jun 2026), PAS terminated cooperation with BERSATU. The 16-seat PAS announcement (FMT + 1media) supersedes this allocation. The Wikipedia table is a snapshot of the pre-break PN structure.

### CM Projection
Wikipedia projects **Akmal Saleh** as potential CM under "PN + BN-UMNO" coalition — consistent with Cycle 3 finding.

### N21 Duyong (Helmy's seat)
PN allocates PAS to contest Duyong — meaning PAS will challenge Helmy directly. This is important for PIR-13: Helmy faces a PAS challenger in his marginal seat.
