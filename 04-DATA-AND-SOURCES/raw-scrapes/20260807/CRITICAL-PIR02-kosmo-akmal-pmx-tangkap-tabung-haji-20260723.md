## Key Quotes

> "Saya mohon kepada YAB PMX untuk tangkap dan dakwa orang yang dikatakan 'sakau' duit tabung haji tersebut"

> "Rakyat berhak untuk tahu siapa yang sakau. RCI terhadap tabung haji telah dilakukan, maka mohon dedah dan tangkap mereka yang sakau duit tabung haji tersebut."

> "Tetapi sehingga hari ini tiada dakwaan telah dibuktikan kepada mana-mana individu. Kepada PMX kami berharap untuk tangkap siapa yang sakau itu."

## Key Details

- **Dr. Akmal Saleh** — UMNO Youth Chief & Merlimau State Assemblyman (ADUN)
- Urged PM Anwar Ibrahim to **arrest and prosecute** individuals who allegedly misappropriated ("sakau") Tabung Haji funds
- RCI (Royal Commission of Inquiry) on Tabung Haji **has been established**
- Akmal claims the issue has been **long discussed** but **no charges** have been proven against any individual to date
- PM Anwar, at an event in Ampangan, Negeri Sembilan, expressed frustration that some parties remain unaware of the misappropriation that occurred previously
- Anwar clarified the misappropriation involved **Tabung Haji and 1MDB** issues
- Akmal's statement was made via a **Facebook post** on 23 July 2026

## Context

- The call comes amid ongoing RCI proceedings on Tabung Haji
- PM Anwar has publicly acknowledged misappropriation occurred in both Tabung Haji and 1MDB cases
