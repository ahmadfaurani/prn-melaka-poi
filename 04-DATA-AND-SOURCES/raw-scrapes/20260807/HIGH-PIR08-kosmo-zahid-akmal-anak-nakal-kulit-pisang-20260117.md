## Key Context

UMNO President **Ahmad Zahid Hamidi** delivered a closing speech at the UMNO General Assembly (PAU 2025) at WTCKL, offering both humorous and cautionary advice to UMNO Youth Chief **Datuk Dr. Muhamad Akmal Saleh**.

## Key Quotes

> *"Akmal ini anak yang baik, cuma sekali-sekala nakal. Bila anak bergaduh, ayah tidak semestinya marah atau hukum. Anak nakal tetap anak yang disayang."*

> *"Sebab itu bila Akmal bercakap lantang, saya tak panggil suruh berhenti. Bila Pemuda nak buat konvensyen, saya izinkan. Kalau anak nakal, Presiden sayang satu kali, Presiden akan sayang dua kali."*

> *"Akmal, ayahanda pun pernah nak jadi Ketua Pemuda UMNO. Ayahanda masuk ICU. Jangan terpijak kulit pisang yang sama."*

*(The last quote was met with laughter and applause from delegates)*

## Main Points

- **Tone:** Joking but cautionary — Zahid described Akmal as a "good but sometimes naughty child" in advocating people's issues
- **Freedom with limits:** The freedom given to UMNO Youth signals **trust and affection** from leadership, **not unlimited leniency**
- **No censorship:** Zahid confirmed he never stopped Akmal from speaking boldly or blocked Youth conventions
- **Warning against repeating past mistakes:** Zahid referenced his own experience aspiring to be Youth Chief and "entering ICU" — cautioning Akmal not to step on the "same banana peel"
- **Youth's role:** Pemuda serves as the **grassroots voice and frontline** of the party, but must remain **grounded in reality** and act within the party's interests
- **Election opportunities:** UMNO consistently gives young candidates chances, including nominating the Youth Chief himself in strategic constituencies

## Analysis — Zahid-Akmal Succession Relationship (PIR-08)

This is the definitive source on the Zahid-Akmal relationship dynamic:
1. **Paternalistic framing** — Zahid positions himself as the "father" and Akmal as the "naughty but loved son," establishing hierarchical authority while granting rhetorical latitude
2. **Explicit endorsement of Akmal's bold speaking** — "saya tak panggil suruh berhenti" confirms Zahid never censored Akmal, validating the rhetorical freedom Akmal enjoys
3. **The ICU/banana peel warning** — Zahid's self-reference to aspiring to be Youth Chief and "entering ICU" is a veiled caution about overreach/health/political risk. This is both protective and constraining
4. **Strategic constituency nomination** — Zahid confirms UMNO nominates the Youth Chief in strategic constituencies (Akmal = Merlimau), linking Youth leadership to electoral positioning
5. **PAU 2025 timing** — This was the closing speech, the authoritative word on the Zahid-Akmal dynamic before the 2026 political season (resignation, Tabung Haji, etc.)
