## Key Development

PAS president Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang declared that **Parti Pribumi Bersatu Malaysia (Bersatu) is no longer a member of Perikatan Nasional (PN)** after Bersatu announced plans to form a new political coalition.

## Key Quotes from Abdul Hadi

> "They (Bersatu) have already stated their intention to want to form another 'team'. They announced it themselves. They are automatically out."

> "There is no need for an official announcement. They have removed themselves by (making such an announcement)."

Hadi stated this after delivering a sermon at **Masjid Rusila in Terengganu**.

## Constitutional Basis

- The **PN constitution** provides that any party choosing to **join or establish another alliance** would **automatically cease to be a member** of the coalition
- Hadi argued no formal PN announcement was needed

## Bersatu's Counterposition

- Bersatu **maintains it is still part of PN**
- Claims the coalition's constitution requires **consensus among all component parties** before any member can be removed
- Bersatu president **Tan Sri Muhyiddin Yassin** announced plans to form a stronger new coalition

## Background Timeline

- **Last month (June 2026):** PAS ended political cooperation with Bersatu
- Both parties remained component members of PN amid **growing tensions**
- **Post-Negeri Sembilan state election:** Muhyiddin announced new coalition plans

## Proposed New Coalition Details

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|

| **Member Parties** | Parti Putra (led by Datuk Ibrahim Ali), Urimai (led by P. Ramasamy) |
