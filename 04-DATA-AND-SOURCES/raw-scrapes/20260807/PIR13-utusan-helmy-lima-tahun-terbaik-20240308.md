## Full Content

**Reporter:** Hadi Ab. Manap

### Subject Profile
- Name: Datuk Mohd. Noor Helmy Abdul Halem
- Position: ADUN Duyong, Melaka
- Role: Timbalan Pengerusi Jawatankuasa Sains, Teknologi, Inovasi dan Komunikasi Digital Melaka
- Elected: PRN Melaka, 20 November 2021
- Nickname: "Mat" (self-styled)
- Profile: New-generation politician known for sharing engaging videos on social media and fast problem-solving

### Key Themes

**1. Philosophy: No False Hope to the People**
"Jangan beri harapan palsu kepada rakyat." — Core principle guiding his approach as a representative.

**2. Social Media Strategy — Three Purposes:**
1. To inform the public that issues are being attended to
2. To educate the community — showing what should/shouldn't be done
3. To demonstrate action — people want to see tangible actions, not just statements

**3. Fast Action & Public Perception:**
- Aware his vocal approach makes him either loved or hated
- Does not care whether he is liked or disliked — results matter most
"Benci atau menghormati tidak penting, pada saya, saya hendak hasil."

**4. Comparison with Previous Duyong Leadership:**
- Respectful of predecessors — each has their own style
- Duyong is an urban-fringe area; much was already established
- Flood issue: People credit him for solving it, but he humbly notes previous leaders may have laid the groundwork
"Tinggal kebetulan pada waktu saya, nampak jalan itu."

**5. Working with CM Ab Rauf:**
- CM described as corporate-style leader, results-oriented, fast-paced
- Helmy finds this compatible
"Beliau laju, kita pun laju. Jangan kita laju, beliau lemau, kita seram. Tapi yang ini beliau laju, kadang kita pula tidak terkejar."

**6. Political Reality:**
- Describes today's politics as "fragile" (rapuh) — no one can claim to be strong
- Public support can change in an instant

**7. Five-Year Self-Assessment:**
"Lima tahun ini saya sudah buat yang terbaik. Kalau hendak kata saya hendak usaha lagi untuk beri yang terbaik, saya sudah tidak mampu, itulah yang terbaik. Kalau saya diberi peluang lagi, saya akan meneruskan apa yang saya ada. Saya tidak boleh kata, saya akan cuba yang terbaik."

## Key Intelligence for PIR-13

1. **Succession positioning:** Helmy's self-assessment reveals a politician who has given his "absolute best" and is satisfied with his performance — not positioning himself as ascending, but as a steady performer. This is consistent with a Timbalan EXCO who may not be aggressively seeking higher office.

2. **CM relationship:** Compatible with Ab Rauf's corporate, results-oriented style. Describes himself as struggling to keep up with CM's pace — suggests Helmy is a follower, not a challenger, in the CM relationship.

3. **Political realism:** "Politics is fragile" — shows awareness of his marginal seat (200 votes). Not overconfident about re-election.

4. **Flood issue as signature achievement:** The Duyong flood problem was solved during his term — a concrete policy deliverable, even if he humbly credits prior groundwork.

5. **Social media as governance tool:** His 3-purpose framework (inform, educate, demonstrate action) is his primary policy delivery mechanism — aligns with his TikTok 1.2M followers and the Malaysiakini "Adun viral" profile.

6. **Duyong profile:** "Urban-fringe area" (pinggir bandar) — not a deep rural seat, which explains the diverse demographic (Chinese, Malay) and the cross-community appeal.
