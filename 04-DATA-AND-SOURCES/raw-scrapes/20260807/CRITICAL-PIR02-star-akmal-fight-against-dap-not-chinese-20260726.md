# Akmal Saleh says his fight is against DAP and Pakatan, not Chinese or Indians

- **Source:** The Star
- **URL:** https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/07/26/akmal-saleh-says-his-fight-is-against-dap-and-pakatan-not-chinese-or-indians
- **Author:** Gerard Gimino
- **Date:** Sunday, 26 Jul 2026, 11:52 PM MYT
- **PIR Tag:** PIR-02 (Rhetoric Trajectory & National Impact), PIR-08 (Succession Positioning)
- **Priority:** CRITICAL

---

## Key Figure
- **Datuk Dr Muhamad Akmal Saleh** — Umno Youth Chief

## Core Message
Akmal Saleh clarified that his political fight is directed at **DAP and Pakatan Harapan**, not against Chinese or Indians as communities.

## Key Quotes

> "We have given it time and we can work together but we draw our lines – do not touch our religion, race or our rulers. If you do, it doesn't matter if you are a friend or foe, we will fight you till the end."

> "If doing so is racist or against the law, I challenge the government to arrest me. I am willing to be arrested if my stance is an offence."

> "If you want to be friends, we can but do not enter my house yet. If you sincerely want to be friends, then we will do so. But if you are not serious, there is no place for you in my house."

## Main Points

### Stance on Unity Government
- Akmal stated his position has been **consistent since the unity government was formed** at the federal level
- He defined clear "red lines": **religion, race, and Malay Rulers** — issues he will defend regardless of whether the challenger is friend or foe

### Umno General Assembly 2023
- Akmal emphasized that **Umno Youth never invited Pakatan leaders** to observe the 2023 Umno general assembly
- Invitations to Pakatan came from **other party wings**, including Wanita and Puteri Umno
- He drew a distinction between his wing's stance and that of other Umno factions

### Challenge on "Racist" Label
- Akmal questioned whether defending the **special position of Malays**, **sovereignty of Malay Rulers**, and **sanctity of Islam** constitutes racism
- He openly **challenged the government to arrest him** if his stance is deemed an offence

### Location & Context
- Statements made during a **ceramah (political talk)** in **Linggi, Port Dickson** on July 26
- Article appears amid political activity surrounding **Negri Sembilan and Melaka state polls**

## Analysis
- Akmal's "not anti-Chinese, anti-DAP" framing is a deliberate repositioning to counter the multiracial-appeal critique (PIR-02)
- The "red lines" (3R: religion, race, rulers) formulation is his consistent rhetorical framework
- The "challenge the government to arrest me" posture mirrors his later Tabung Haji rhetoric (23 Jul) — a pattern of daring the establishment to act against him
- Made in Port Dickson (NS), linking to the NS/Melaka state poll corridor
