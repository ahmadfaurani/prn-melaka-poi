## Full Content

### Key Quotes

> "Alhamdulilah Allah makbulkan doa saya apabila sedikit demi sedikit Allah tunjukkan perangai sebenar mereka. Cukup-cukuplah kita nak bekerjasama dengan mereka ini. Kalau titah Sultan pun sanggup diperlekehkan oleh kerana babi, apa lagi yang lain."

> "Keputusan di Johor wajib untuk dilaksanakan ke semua negeri."

### Summary

#### Main Points

- **Datuk Dr. Muhammad Akmal Saleh** (Ketua Pemuda UMNO) criticized certain parties for:
  - Disrespecting and mocking royal decrees (*titah institusi diraja*)
  - Showing their "true colors" gradually

- He stated that cooperation with those who disobey the royal institution **must be stopped**

- He urged UMNO leadership to **go solo** in all upcoming:
  - Pilihan Raya Negeri (PRN) — State Elections
  - Pilihan Raya Umum (PRU) — General Elections

- He emphasized that **Johor's decision** (to contest solo) must be implemented in **all states**

### BN Johor's Decision

- **BN Johor confirmed** contesting all **56 DUN (State Legislative Assembly) seats** in the upcoming PRN
- Purpose: To defend their mandate and continue state development agenda
- **Datuk Onn Hafiz Ghazi** (Pengerusi BN Johor) stated the decision was made after:
  - Considering public opinion
  - Listening to UMNO grassroots voices
  - Discussions with party leadership

### Context

- The statement appears to be a response to parties that dismissed royal decrees, with Dr. Akmal framing this as a religious sign that these parties' true nature is being revealed
- The remark "oleh kerana babi" (because of pork) suggests the royal decree controversy involved issues related to pork/livestock
- This signals a potential shift in UMNO/BN's political alliance strategy toward independent contesting

## Intelligence Analysis Notes

Key observations: (a) Akmal is invoking religious framing ("Allah tunjukkan perangai sebenar") and royal institution defense ("titah Sultan diperlekehkan") — this is a notable escalation from his anti-DAP rhetoric to a broader moral/religious authority framing; (b) the "babi" (pork) reference suggests a specific controversy involving a party that dismissed a Sultan's decree related to pork/livestock — likely DAP given the context; (c) Akmal is now advocating UMNO go SOLO in ALL states — this directly contradicts the BN-PN coalition that was later formed for Johor/NS PRN. This means Akmal's "go solo" position was OVERRULED by the party leadership (Zahid formed BN-PN coalitions). This is significant for PIR-08: it shows Akmal's policy positions are not always adopted by the party, but he continues advocating them — positioning himself as the "true UMNO voice" independent of leadership compromises. Note: the "go solo" position was NOT adopted for Johor/NS, where BN-PN coalitions won. The subsequent coalition success weakens Akmal's solo-contest argument.
