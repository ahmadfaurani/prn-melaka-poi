## Intelligence Analysis Notes

[ASSESSMENT] This NS article is a comparator for Melaka federal-state dynamics. Zahid's definitive "tidak akan pernah ada" (will never happen) for PH Exco in NS establishes the BN-PN coalition model: BN leads, PN participates, PH excluded. This is the model that Melaka may follow. Key contrast with Melaka: in Melaka, DAP/PH was in a "semangat persefahaman" (informal understanding) that was never formal — and has now exited. In NS, PH was never in government. The NS model (BN-PN, no PH) is what Akmal advocated in May ("go solo" / stop cooperating with those who disrespect royalty). The fact that NS adopted BN-PN (not solo) shows Akmal's "go solo" position was overruled again.
