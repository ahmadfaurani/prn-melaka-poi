### Main Summary
Adam Adli firmly denies ever changing his stance or making a "U-turn" regarding PTPTN (National Higher Education Fund) loan issues. He maintains he has remained **consistent and firm** with his principles from the beginning.

### New PTPTN Initiatives Implemented (2026)
1. **Targeted repayment system** based on borrower's ability to pay
2. **Income-contingent repayment element** now incorporated — allowing fairer leniency and payment deferments
3. **Travel restrictions no longer applied blanket-style** — now based on income assessment

> "Sekatan perjalanan sekarang tidak dibuat secara melulu seperti dahulu di mana semua orang dikenakan tindakan tanpa mengira latar belakang. Sekarang kita lihat kepada pendapatan. Adakah mereka berpendapatan RM6,000 ke atas dan berada dalam golongan mampu bayar? Kita tidak lagi laksanakan sekatan secara blanket (menyeluruh)."

**Income threshold:** RM6,000+ (considered capable of repayment)
