## Full Content

**Date & Location:** 8 April 2026, Melaka

**Event:** Deputy Minister of Defence, **YB Adly Zahari**, officiated the launch of the **Ikhtiar Autisme Angkatan Tentera Malaysia (IA-ATM)** programme at **Pusat Pemulihan Dalam Komuniti (PPDK) Umi, Kem Terendak, Melaka**.

### Key Facts

- **Purpose:** Continuous effort by the Ministry of Defence to ensure welfare and well-being of autistic children among Defence personnel families, while increasing societal awareness of social responsibility toward special needs groups
- **Second launch location** — first was at **Pangkalan TLDM Lumut, Perak** (his visit to Melaka was the SECOND site of the programme)
- **Collaborating partners:**
  - Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat (JKM)
  - National Autism Society of Malaysia (NASOM)
  - **Funding support:** Lembaga Tabung Haji (TH) as part of corporate social responsibility
- **Target group:** Autistic children aged **3 to 15 years**
- **Programme focus:**
  - Development of potential through specialised learning modules and therapy
  - Strengthening strategic cooperation networks between Ministry of Defence, government agencies, GLCs, GLICs, and NGOs
  - Providing comprehensive support to special needs children
