# Melaka Won't Fill PH Vacancies After Coalition Exits State Government

## Key Summary

The Melaka State Government will **not fill positions vacated by Pakatan Harapan (PH) representatives** following the coalition's withdrawal from the state Cabinet, announced Chief Minister **Datuk Seri Ab Rauf Yusoh**.

## Vacated Positions (all automatically vacated, will remain unfilled)

- State Executive Council (Exco) members
- Local authority councillors
- Village Development and Security Committee (JPKK) members

## Key Quotes — Ab Rauf Yusoh (CM)

> "As reported by the media, the positions are automatically vacated. We will not make any new appointments because there's not much time left to serve."

> "That is their choice. If they have decided not to remain with the state government, we accept it with an open heart. We do not want this issue to become a platform for hostility or personal attacks. Differences of opinion are acceptable, but they should be expressed professionally and in a manner that is acceptable to the public."

## Reasoning for Not Filling Vacancies

- Only a short period remains in the state's current term
- No new appointments due to limited time left to serve

## PH Withdrawal Context

- **Yesterday (16 Jul):** Melaka PH announced final decision to withdraw from state administration
- **Reason:** PH rejected amendments to the Melaka State Constitution Enactment to introduce appointed state assembly seats
- Decision endorsed at meeting involving coalition leadership and all PH state assembly members

## PH Leadership Joint Statement (signatories)

- **Adly Zahari** — Melaka PH Chairman
- **Adam Adli Abdul Halim** — Acting Melaka PKR Chairman
- **Khoo Poay Tiong** — Melaka DAP Chairman
- **Datuk Ashraf Mukhlis Minghat** — Melaka Amanah Chairman

## BN-PN Cooperation Possibility (NEW — key PIR-05 signal)

- Ab Rauf noted he has **yet to see** a BN-PN arrangement in Melaka similar to developments in Negeri Sembilan
- He **did not rule out** the possibility of better understanding between BN and PN in the state

## State Administration History

- Functioned well during nearly three years of cooperation with PH
- Differing political positions led PH to chart its own course

## Event Context

Ab Rauf made these statements after attending the **Hari Exco Bersama Rakyat (HEBAT) programme** in **Merlimau, Jasin**.
