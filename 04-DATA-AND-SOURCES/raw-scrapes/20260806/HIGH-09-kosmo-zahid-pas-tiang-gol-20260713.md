## Full Content

### Key Information

- **Speaker:** Datuk Seri Dr. Ahmad Zahid Hamidi — President of UMNO & Deputy Prime Minister
- **Context:** Press conference after the Luncheon Session with the TVET@KKDW Contingent heading to WorldSkills Shanghai 2026, Kuala Lumpur
- **Subject:** Warning against trusting PAS's political statements; clarifying BN-PAS relationship status

### Key Quotes

> "Apa yang dinyatakan itu tidak semuanya harus diterima sebagai satu kenyataan yang tepat kerana kita tidak ada sebarang perjanjian dengan Pas. Cuma kalau ada perbincangan pun sekadar persefahaman dan kita tidak mengutamakan sebarang perbincangan itu."

> "Jadi jangan positif sangatlah dengan mereka ini. Cuma kita harus bersangka baik sahaja, tetapi jangan terlalu positif. Mereka boleh tukar tiang gol bila-bila masa sahaja. Jadi saya anggap bahawa itu kenyataan politik oleh mereka."

> "Itu sebab saya nyatakan, kata-kata mereka ini kita jangan pegang sangat sebab mereka boleh berubah. Banyak kali, banyak parti dah kena dengan mereka ini. Jadi saya harap mereka tidak berubahlah."

### Summary of Main Points

- No formal agreement exists between BN and PAS; any discussions are merely "understandings" and are not prioritized by BN
- Zahid warned all parties not to be overly confident in PAS's statements, claiming the party can change its stance at any time ("tukar tiang gol" / move the goalposts)
- He cited the Johor State Election (PRN Johor) experience as evidence — PAS leaders' prior statements differed from the actual situation during candidate nomination and voting day
- PAS cannot be relied upon as a partner because their historical record shows they frequently change positions
- Many other parties have been burned by PAS's shifting stances in the past

### Background / Trigger

PAS President Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang had reportedly hinted at the existence of discussions regarding political cooperation and seat negotiations between PAS and BN ahead of the Negeri Sembilan State Election (PRN).

### Analysis Note (PIR-06 Relevance)

Zahid's caution about PAS reliability is directly relevant to Ab Rauf's "BN Plus" strategy. If Ab Rauf opens negotiations with PN/PAS for Melaka PRN, Zahid's "tiang gol" warning suggests federal UMNO leadership retains skepticism about PAS as a coalition partner. This creates potential tension between Ab Rauf's open-door coalition approach and Zahid's cautious stance. The fact that Zahid is DPM and UMNO President while Ab Rauf is state BN chairman means this federal-state dynamic is a live variable for PIR-06.
