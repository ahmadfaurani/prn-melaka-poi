## Full Content

### Key Development

- **Datuk Seri Shamsul Iskandar Mohd Akin** stood down as Chairman of PKR's Melaka State Leadership Council
- **Adam Adli Abdul Halim** (MP for Hang Tuah Jaya) appointed **Acting Chairman**, effective **15 December 2025**
- Adam Adli previously served as **Deputy Chairman** of the council

### Official Statements

**Shamsul Iskandar (via Facebook, Dec 17):**
> "I will continue to focus on strengthening the party divisions and contributing to the overall strength of PKR in Melaka."

> "I give my full support to brother Adam Adli in carrying out his trust as Acting Chairman of the Melaka State Leadership Council and believe that he is capable of leading well."

- Described the decision as being made **in accordance with the party's constitution and long-established traditions**
- Called on party leaders to remain **united, mature and disciplined** amid political challenges and attacks on the party president's leadership

**Dr Fuziah Salleh (PKR Secretary-General):**
- Decision followed the party's move to **temporarily relieve** Shamsul Iskandar of his duties
- Adam Adli to serve as Acting Chairman **until further notice**
- Party priority: **strengthening organisational structure** and **mobilising grassroots machinery**
- Key focus: Ensuring PKR remains **relevant and closely connected to the people of Melaka**

### Upcoming Milestones (2026)

- **PKR party congress**
- **Melaka State Legislative Assembly election**

### Context

- Shamsul Iskandar's title: **Former Senior Political Secretary**
- The leadership transition is framed as ensuring **leadership continuity** and **smooth functioning** of the party at state level
- Decision made by PKR's **Political Bureau**
