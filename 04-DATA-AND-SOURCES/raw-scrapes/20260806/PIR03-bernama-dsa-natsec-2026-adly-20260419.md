## Full Content

### Event Overview
- **Event:** Defence Services Asia (DSA) and National Security Asia (NatSec) 2026, 19th edition
- **Dates:** April 20-23, 2026
- **Location:** Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre (MITEC), Kuala Lumpur
- **Theme:** "Empowering Capabilities and Resilience Through Technology"
- **Officiated by:** PM Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim

### Record Participation
- **1,456 exhibitors** from **63 countries** (up from 1,324/60 in 2024)
- **37 national pavilions**
- **First-time participants:** Croatia, Finland, Hungary, Japan, Russia

### Local Industry Focus
- **368 Malaysian companies** participating via newly introduced **Malaysian Defence Industry Consortium pavilion**
- Aligns with **National Defence Industry Policy (DIPN/DIPKN)** targeting **30% local component usage** in defence assets
> "The 30 per cent local component target is achievable. We are confident because many local companies have come forward with products that can be considered by OEMs." — Defence Minister Khaled Nordin

### Industrial Collaboration Programme (ICP)
- Defence Ministry to present ICP documents worth **estimated RM1.4 billion** to selected companies
- Focus: Technology and expertise transfer; maintenance, component manufacturing, asset production

### Geopolitical Context
- **Iranian exhibitors withdrew** due to logistical challenges in Strait of Hormuz
- **Strait of Malacca** situation contrasted with Hormuz — Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore uphold international law and freedom of navigation/trade

### Malaysia's Non-Aligned Stance
> "Unlike some countries where such exhibitions are attended only by aligned nations, Malaysia provides a neutral platform where countries involved in conflicts are also present." — Khaled Nordin
