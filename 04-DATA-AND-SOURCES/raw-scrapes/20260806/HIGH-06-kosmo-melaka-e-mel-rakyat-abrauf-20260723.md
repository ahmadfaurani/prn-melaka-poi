## Full Content

### Key Facts

- Melaka is the first state in Malaysia to launch the E-Mel Rakyat initiative as the official government communication platform
- Uses official domain: @melaka.my
- Launched by Chief Minister Datuk Seri Ab. Rauf Yusoh
- Venue: Pusat Dagangan Antarabangsa Melaka (MITC), Ayer Keroh

### Purpose & Rationale

The initiative aims to deliver official government information directly, accurately, and securely to citizens without relying on social media platforms that often spread false information.

> "Saya sendiri menerima aduan daripada rakyat yang menerima mesej kononnya menawarkan bantuan RM100 daripada kerajaan negeri, namun apabila pautan itu dibuka, ia sebenarnya cubaan mendapatkan maklumat peribadi mereka."

> "Atas sebab itu, kerajaan negeri memperkenalkan E-Mel Rakyat melalui domain rasmi @melaka.my sebagai saluran komunikasi yang lebih tersusun, selamat dan diyakini."
> — Datuk Seri Ab. Rauf Yusoh, Chief Minister of Melaka

### Registration Statistics (as of 22 July 2026)

| Category | Accounts Registered |
|----------|---------------------|
| Total accounts | >203,000 |
| Students, teachers, parents & guardians | >193,000 |

### Platform Functions

The E-Mel Rakyat platform delivers directly to target groups:
- Government aid information
- Education opportunities
- Employment information
- Welfare initiatives

> "Menerusi platform ini, pelbagai maklumat berkaitan bantuan kerajaan, peluang pendidikan, pekerjaan serta inisiatif kebajikan dapat disalurkan terus kepada kumpulan sasar tanpa melalui perantara."

### Future Targets

- Goal: Activate E-Mel Rakyat accounts for 85,000 household heads across 618 traditional villages statewide
- Aspiration: "1 Rumah, 1 E-Mel Rakyat" (1 House, 1 E-Mel Rakyat)
