## Full Content

### Key Quote

> "Kalau undi Melayu tidak lagi berpecah, kita juga boleh menang dan rampas semula Selangor."
> — Khairy Jamaluddin Abu Bakar, Bekas Ketua Pemuda UMNO

### Summary

#### BN-PN Cooperation Formula
- Khairy proposed that cooperation between Barisan Nasional (BN) and Perikatan Nasional (PN) could be the key to winning back Selangor
- The formula is currently being tested in the Negeri Sembilan State Election (PRN)
- Selangor shares similar voter demographics and profiles with Negeri Sembilan, making the formula potentially applicable

#### Conditions for Application
- Must wait for PRN Negeri Sembilan results before any leadership discussions between both parties
- Requires sincerity and commitment from both sides
- Test whether BN supporters can vote for PAS candidates

#### Assessment of Current Government Partnership
- Khairy stated there is "something wrong" with BN's cooperation with parties in the Unity Government
- Issues are "not minor but numerous"
- BN has been given 4 years in the Federal Government partnership

#### Position on Government Exit
- BN should NOT exit the current government at this time
- National stability must be prioritized until the next General Election (PRU)

### Context
- Statement made at the Mesyuarat Perwakilan Wanita, Pemuda dan Puteri UMNO Bahagian Subang in Shah Alam
- Khairy is the former UMNO Youth Chief and former MP for Rembau
- The cooperation would benefit BN, PAS, and Wawasan through unified Malay votes

### Analysis Note (PIR-02/06 Relevance)

Khairy — Akmal's predecessor as UMNO Youth Chief — provides a more measured version of the BN-PN coalition argument. While Akmal frames it as "DAP fortress cracking" and multiracial triumph, Khairy frames it as Malay vote consolidation + pragmatic governance critique. Both converge on BN-PN cooperation but from different rhetorical positions. Khairy's "something wrong with Unity Government" critique aligns with Akmal's motions but is delivered with Establishment restraint. Relevant to PIR-02 (rhetoric trajectory comparison) and PIR-06 (coalition dynamics). Khairy's "BN should NOT exit federal government" contrasts with Akmal's earlier motion to exit — showing the internal UMNO debate spectrum.
