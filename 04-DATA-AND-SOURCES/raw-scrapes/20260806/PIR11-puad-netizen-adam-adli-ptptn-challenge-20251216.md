## Full Content (The Rakyat Insight)

### Key Summary
UMNO Supreme Council member **Datuk Dr. Mohd. Puad Zarkashi** challenged newly-appointed **Timbalan Menteri Pengajian Tinggi Adam Adli Abdul Halim** to fulfill his former student activist pledge to abolish PTPTN loans.

### Appointment Context
- **Cabinet reshuffle** announced by PM Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim
- Adam Adli **transferred** from Deputy Minister of Youth and Sports → Deputy Minister of Higher Education
- **Replaced:** Datuk Mustapha Sakmud
- Adam Adli is **MP for Hang Tuah Jaya**

### Puad's Challenge
- Described the appointment as "most fitting" given Adam Adli's background and past struggles
- Congratulated Adam Adli but used the occasion to **challenge him** on his PTPTN abolition agenda
- Urged Adam Adli to **bravely raise** the PTPTN abolition issue openly in post-Cabinet meetings at MOHE

### Sarcasm & Reminder
> "Menjadi pembangkang jauh lebih mudah berbanding memikul tanggungjawab apabila sudah berada dalam kerajaan." (Being in opposition is far easier than bearing responsibility when already in government.)

### Background
- Known as one of the **most vocal student leaders** in PKR's reform movement
- Specifically prominent in **demanding PTPTN abolition** during his student era
- Now faces the irony of being in government after advocating against the institution he now oversees
