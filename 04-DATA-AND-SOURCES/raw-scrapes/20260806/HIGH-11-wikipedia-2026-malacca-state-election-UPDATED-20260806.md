### Background Timeline — UPDATED

#### July 2026: PH Withdraws from Government (Updated Detail)
- **14 July 2026:** DAP withdrew from state government to oppose BN's move to amend state Constitution allowing 7 unelected assembly members
- Constitutional amendment passed 23-5 (all 21 BN + 2 PN voted for; all 5 PH voted against)
- DAP representatives moved to opposition benches on 15 July
- State PH chief Adly Zahari (Amanah) initially remained with government side
- **16 July:** PH leadership meeting confirmed departure, agreed by all 3 component parties (PKR, DAP, Amanah)
- PM Anwar Ibrahim had called for DAP to defer departure, but was overruled

#### Preparations
- **17 May 2026:** Chief Minister disclosed election could be held within 120 days
- **15 July:** Bersama party announced it would not contest Negeri Sembilan election, focusing on Malacca instead
- Seat negotiations among opposition parties expected to be critical
