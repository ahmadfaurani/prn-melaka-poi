## Extracted Content (Wikipedia summary)

**Mas Ermieyati binti Samsudin** (born 13 November 1976) — Malaysian politician and lawyer currently serving as **Chairperson of the Public Accounts Committee (PAC)** since 4 April 2023 and **MP for Masjid Tanah** since May 2013. She is the **second female PAC Chairperson** in Malaysian history (after Noraini Ahmad).
