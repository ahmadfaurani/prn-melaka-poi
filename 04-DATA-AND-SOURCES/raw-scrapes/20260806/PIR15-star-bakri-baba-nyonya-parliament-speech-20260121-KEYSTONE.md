### Key Quote

### Main Proposals by MP Bakri Jamaluddin

1. **Designate Baba Nyonya and Chetty communities as national unity icons**
2. **Include community members in the National Unity Advisory Council** — "This way, they can contribute further towards unity"
3. **Appoint community members as Senators**

### Historical Context Provided

**Baba Nyonya (Peranakan Chinese):**
- Arrived in Melaka in the **early 15th century**, during the **Melaka Sultanate era**
- Assimilated into local communities, reflected through **food, clothing, and strong command of the Malay language**
- Origins traced to as early as the **12th century** when merchants from southern China sailed for trade
- Shaped by **Chinese, Malay, and colonial influences**
- Described as "one of Southeast Asia's most unique cultural communities"

**Chetty (Indian Peranakans):**
- Also arrived in Melaka in the early 15th century
- Primarily centred in **Melaka**
- Unique culture blending **South Indian, Malay, and Chinese influences**
