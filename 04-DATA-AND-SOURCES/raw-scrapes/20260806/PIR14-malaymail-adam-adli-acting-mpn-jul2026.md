## Full Content

### Key Facts

- **Issue:** State Constitution (Melaka) (Amendment) Enactment 2026 regarding appointment of **nominated Members of the Legislative Assembly (ADUN)**
- **Trigger:** Melaka DAP announced **immediate withdrawal** from the state government after the State Legislative Assembly **passed the amendment** allowing nominated assemblymen
- **Five PH assemblymen** took a stance against the enactment and decided to withdraw from the state government
- **PM Anwar Ibrahim** requested Melaka DAP to **defer its decision** to withdraw, to focus on development and people's welfare

### Key Statements

**Adam Adli Abdul Halim** — Acting Melaka Keadilan State Leadership Council (MPN) Chairman & Deputy Higher Education Minister:

> "Melaka Keadilan continues to hold the principle that political and administrative stability is crucial to guaranteeing state progress, economic growth, and the people's well-being."

> "At the same time, the proposal for nominated assemblymen must be carefully evaluated based on the principles of accountability, integrity, and democratic spirit."

> "The space for discussion must remain open so that every decision can be reached maturely, responsibly, and for the benefit of the people of Melaka."

### PKR's Position

- **Urges all parties** not to make hasty decisions that could jeopardize administrative stability, public welfare, and state development
- Calls for the issue to be resolved in the **spirit of consensus and negotiation**
- **Respects** the stance of the five PH assemblymen
- Notes the withdrawal decision was **not mutually agreed** at the Melaka PH leadership level
- Upholds PM Anwar's stance that differences of opinion must be resolved through **consensus, prudent negotiation, and prioritising people's interests**

### Context

The controversy centers on a constitutional amendment enabling the appointment of nominated assemblymen in Melaka. DAP's withdrawal from the state government followed the passage of this amendment, creating political tension within the PH coalition. PKR is positioning itself as a mediator, advocating for dialogue over unilateral action.
