## Summary

Dr. Muhamad Akmal Saleh (UMNO Youth Chief & ADUN Merlimau) urged PM Anwar Ibrahim to arrest and prosecute individuals who allegedly misappropriated ("sakau") Tabung Haji funds. A Royal Commission of Inquiry (RCI) on Tabung Haji has been established.

## Key Quotes

> "Saya mohon kepada YAB PMX untuk tangkap dan dakwa orang yang dikatakan 'sakau' duit tabung haji tersebut. Rakyat berhak untuk tahu siapa yang sakau. RCI terhadap tabung haji telah dilakukan, maka mohon dedah dan tangkap mereka yang sakau duit tabung haji tersebut."

> "Tetapi sehingga hari ini tiada dakwaan telah dibuktikan kepada mana-mana individu. Kepada PMX kami berharap untuk tangkap siapa yang sakau itu."

## Main Points

- Akmal urged PM Anwar to arrest and prosecute individuals who allegedly misappropriated Tabung Haji funds
- A Royal Commission of Inquiry (RCI) on Tabung Haji has been established
- Akmal emphasized that the public has the right to know who misappropriated the funds
- He noted the issue has been discussed for a long time but no charges have been proven against any individual to date
- PM Anwar, at an event in Ampangan, Negeri Sembilan, expressed regret that some parties remain unaware of the misappropriation that occurred previously
- Anwar clarified the misappropriation involves both Tabung Haji and 1MDB issues
