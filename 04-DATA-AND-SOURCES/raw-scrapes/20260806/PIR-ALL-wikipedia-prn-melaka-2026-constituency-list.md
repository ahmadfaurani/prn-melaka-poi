## Key Observations

1. **BN Dominance:** BN holds 20 of 28 seats (71.4%), well above the 15-seat majority threshold
2. **UMNO Strength:** UMNO alone controls 17 seats, the largest single-party presence
3. **PH Weakness:** PH holds only 5 seats, primarily in urban/DAP strongholds (4 DAP + 1 AMANAH)
4. **PN Presence:** PN has 3 seats (PAS N06 Rembia + BERSATU N11 Sungai Udang + WAWASAN N24 Bemban)
5. **Projected Change:** Article suggests potential shift with Muhamad Akmal Saleh becoming CM under PN+BN coalition
6. **Mohd Noor Helmy (N21 Duyong):** Confirmed as BN-UMNO incumbent — corroborates Cycle 2 finding
