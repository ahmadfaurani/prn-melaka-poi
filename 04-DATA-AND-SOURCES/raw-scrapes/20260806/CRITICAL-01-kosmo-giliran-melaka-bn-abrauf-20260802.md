## Full Content

### Key Context

- BN won big in the Negeri Sembilan State Election (PRN), results announced the day before
- This victory has boosted morale for BN Melaka machinery to defend the state government in upcoming PRN expected this year
- Datuk Seri Ab. Rauf Yusoh is Chairman of BN Melaka (and current Chief Minister)

### Key Quotes from Ab. Rauf

> "Kita semalam dah dengar keputusan (PRN) di Negeri Sembilan. Cantik kan? Jadi apa salahnya kalau disimbah (kemenangan) ke Melaka sikit, boleh? Siapa yang nak menyimbah ni? Kamu oranglah yang simbah."

> "Tak payah datang cari saya dekat pejabat (biar) saya datang jumpa kamu semua dan cakaplah apa masalah yang ada."

On maintaining political stability and development:

> "Kita umpama pohon yang rendang. Bila pohon rendang, buahnya banyak. Hujan panas boleh berteduh, perut lapar pun boleh dimakan. Tapi janganlah tebang pohon ini. Baja dia, jaga dia... Kalau benda dah cantik, jaga elok-elok."

### Strategic Approach

- Ab. Rauf emphasized going directly to the people (especially villagers) through the Jelajah Ketua Menteri program rather than having them come to his office
- Urged citizens to preserve political stability and development achieved by the state
- Used the metaphor of a fruitful tree providing shade and food — urging people not to "cut it down" but to nurture and protect it
