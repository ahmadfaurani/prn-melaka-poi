### Key Development

**Datuk Mas Ermieyati Samsudin** has resigned as **Bersatu Masjid Tanah division chief**, along with **39 other division committee members**, officially dissolving the division's committee.

A special **two-hour meeting** was held, attended by division committee members, wing chiefs, and branch chiefs.

> "The special meeting also unanimously agreed that Bersatu's Masjid Tanah division had lost its trust in Tan Sri Muhyiddin Yassin as party president."
> "With our resignations, Bersatu's Masjid Tanah committee is officially dissolved."

Mas Ermieyati **confirmed** her and other leaders' resignations when contacted.
