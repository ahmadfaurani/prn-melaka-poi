## Key Points

- **BN Chairman Datuk Seri Dr. Ahmad Zahid Hamidi** hinted that the BN-PN understanding/cooperation may continue to the **Melaka State Election (PRN Melaka)** following their success in Negeri Sembilan
- The mandate received in Negeri Sembilan is a continuation of the victory in **Johor**
- Believed capable of strengthening public support ahead of the next state election

## Key Quote

> "Mandat dan keputusan ini akan terus membawa satu gelombang bagi kami meneruskan gelombang biru dan biru pekat yang telah bermula di Negeri Johor. Insya-Allah ia akan menular ke negeri Melaka."

## PRN Negeri Sembilan Results (Unofficial)

| Coalition | DUN Seats Won |
|-----------|---------------|
| **BN** | 18 |
| **PN** | 7 |
| **Total Combined** | **25** |

- Combined **25 seats** out of **36 total DUN seats** = **more than two-thirds majority**
- Results announced at Wisma UMNO Negeri Sembilan

## Context

- "Gelombang biru" (blue wave) refers to BN's political momentum
- "Biru pekat" (deep blue) likely refers to PN's contribution to the coalition
- The wave began in Johor, continued to Negeri Sembilan, and is expected to spread to Melaka
