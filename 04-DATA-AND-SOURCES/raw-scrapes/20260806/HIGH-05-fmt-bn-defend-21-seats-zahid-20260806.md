### Key statements (extracted)
- **BN chairman Ahmad Zahid Hamidi** announced that **Barisan Nasional will defend all 21 state seats in Melaka**.

- BN remains **open to reaching an understanding with any party, including PN**, as was done in Negeri Sembilan.

### Analytical Significance (PIR-05)
- BN's formal opening position: **defend all 21 seats** (the seats BN won in 2021; current incumbency is 20 after 1 defection to PN).
- "Open to PN understanding" = BN prefers a negotiated pact (avoiding three-cornered fights) but on its terms (BN keeps its seats, PN contests the rest).
- This directly conflicts with PN's "equal share" demand (~14 seats). The negotiation gap is now explicit from both sides.
- Ab Rauf (POI) is the state-level conveyor of this position — directly relevant to his CM/BN leadership role.
- Note the discrepancy: BN says "defend all 21" but current composition is 20 (one BN seat — Sungai Udang, Mohd Aleef Yusof — defected to PN/BERSATU). "21" refers to the 2021 election result; the 22nd-28th seats are PH (5) + PN (3).
