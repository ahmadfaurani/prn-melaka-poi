## Full Content

### Key Figure
- **Datuk Seri Shamsul Iskandar Mohd Akin** — Pengerusan Majlis Pimpinan Negeri (MPN) PKR Melaka & Setiausaha Politik Perdana Menteri

### Core Message: Reject Internal Conflict

The phrase **'pisau di kiri'** (knife on the left) was used as a stern reminder to PKR Melaka members to:
- **Reject internal disputes** and strengthen unity after party elections
- **Eliminate "hidden weapons"** or suspicion among members post-election
- **Avoid factionalism** and petty grievances ("perkara titik bengik")
- **Prepare solidly** for the **Melaka State Election (PRN Melaka)** next year

### Key Quote:
> "Kalau kita benar-benar mahu bermuafakat, tidak perlu ada pihak yang membawa 'pisau di kiri' atau membangkitkan perkara titik bengik (perkara kecil). Oleh itu, kita sebagai parti reformasi perlu berani melakukan perubahan."

### Event: 27 Tahun Reformasi Celebration

- **Organizer:** MPN Melaka
- **Venue:** A hotel in Bandar Melaka, Saturday night (20 Sep 2025)
- **Attendance:** Hundreds of grassroots members
- **Significance:** Commemorates **Reformasi 1998**
- **Future Plan:** This commemoration will become an **annual event in Melaka**

### Three Main Objectives of the Program

1. **Appreciate early reformasi fighters** through contributions
2. **Retrace the footsteps** of the reform movement's struggle
3. **Unite allies from various parties** who have worked together since the Reformasi era

### Cross-Party Unity Highlighted

- The event brought together reform movement veterans across party lines
- **Example cited:** Datuk Hassan Baharom — early Parti Keadilan pioneer in Melaka, now with **Parti Amanah**, yet still attended the event

## Significance for PIR-14

This article documents Shamsul's status **BEFORE the corruption charges** (which occurred 4 Dec 2025). Key timeline insight:

- **21 Sep 2025:** Shamsul was still MPN Chairman AND Senior Political Secretary to PM (dual role), actively leading PKR Melaka grassroots mobilization for the upcoming PRN. He was the public face of PKR Melaka reform movement.
- **31 Jul 2025:** Appointed PKR Melaka Chairman (per Cycle 2 Wikipedia finding)
- **4 Dec 2025:** Charged with 4 corruption counts
- **15 Dec 2025:** Dropped from MPN Chairman (per Malaysiakini)

This article captures the "before" state — Shamsul at the peak of his PKR Melaka leadership, ~10 weeks before his downfall. His reformasi speech shows he was actively building grassroots unity and preparing the PKR Melaka machinery for the PRN. The contrast with his removal 12 weeks later is stark.
