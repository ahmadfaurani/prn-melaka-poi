## PART A — Adly Zahari Biography (Wikipedia, updated 2026-08-06)

**Adly bin Zahari** (born 15 February 1971, age 55), Kampung Pulau, Durian Tunggal, Malacca. Electrical engineer (BEng UTM). AMANAH (since 2015; formerly PAS).

### Current Positions
- **Deputy Minister of Defence** (since 10 December 2022) — under Minister Mohamed Khaled Nordin (since 2023; previously Mohamad Hasan 2022–2023)
- **MP for Alor Gajah** (since 19 Nov 2022) — majority 890
- **MLA for Bukit Katil, Malacca** (since 9 May 2018) — sole AMANAH MLA in Malacca
- **Vice President of AMANAH** (since 5 Sep 2021)
- **State Chairman of PH – Kelantan** (since 5 Nov 2024)
- **State Chairman of PH – Malacca** (since 30 Aug 2017)
- **Treasurer of Pakatan Harapan** (coalition-level)

### 11th Chief Minister of Malacca (11 May 2018 – 9 March 2020)
- Historic first-ever transition of power in Malacca; PH won 15/28 seats
- Rejected a Datukship offer during tenure
