## Ringkasan Utama

PAS akan bertanding di **16 kerusi Dewan Undangan Negeri (DUN)** dalam Pilihan Raya Negeri (PRN) Melaka, meninggalkan baki **12 kerusi** untuk komponen Perikatan Nasional (PN) yang lain — Bersatu dan Gerakan.

## Implikasi Politik (Analisis CJ-MLK-03)

- **PAS menguasai majoriti kerusi** (16 daripada 28) dalam peruntukan PN Melaka — PAS is the dominant PN component in Melaka seat claim
- **12 kerusi untuk Bersatu & Gerakan (kini + WAWASAN)** — but Bersatu in Melaka has collapsed (Mas Ermieyati suspended, divisions dissolved; WAWASAN took over N24 Bemban)
- **Banyak kerusi PAS claim bertindih dengan kerusi BN incumbent** — termasuk kerusi CM Ab Rauf (Tanjung Bidara), Akmal (Merlimau), dan EXCO members. This indicates EITHER: (a) PAS intends to contest vs BN if no seat deal; OR (b) negotiating position for BN-PN seat-sharing
- **Post-NS context:** After NS BN-PN success (1 Aug), Zahid signaled "blue wave" to Melaka; Sanusi said BN-PN Melaka "depends on conditions (waqi)" (CVS-MLK-132); Hadi Awang said Melaka cooperation "not necessarily like Johor/NS" (Sinar 4 Aug). The 16-seat PAS claim is the baseline negotiating position predating the NS momentum.
- **Seat overlap = the core PIR-05 tension:** PAS 16 seats include 7+ BN-held seats. If BN-PN deal materializes, PAS would yield these; if not, three-cornered fights benefit PH.
