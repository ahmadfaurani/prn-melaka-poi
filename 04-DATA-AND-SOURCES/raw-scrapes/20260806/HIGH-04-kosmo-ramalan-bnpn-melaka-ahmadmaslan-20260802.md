## Full Content

### Main Subject
**Datuk Seri Dr. Ahmad Maslan** — Ahli Majlis Kerja Tertinggi (MKT) UMNO & Ahli Parlimen Pontian

### Key Quotes

> "Johor saya ramal 45, dapat 48 kerusi. Negeri Sembilan saya ramal 25, dapat 25. Maka Melaka saya ramal BN-PN menang."

> "Saya difahamkan begitu kerana PH telah menarik diri di Melaka. Oleh itu BN dan PN, sebagaimana yang diberitahu oleh Pengerusi BN, persefahaman ini akan diteruskan di Melaka. Itu bukan kenyataan saya, saya mengulang kenyataan beliau."

### Key Points

- BN-PN coalition predicted to win upcoming PRN Melaka (Melaka State Election)
- Ahmad Maslan's confidence based on his accurate prediction track record:
  - Johor: Predicted 45 seats → Won 48
  - Negeri Sembilan: Predicted 25 seats → Won 25
- Pakatan Harapan (PH) withdrew from cooperation in Melaka, opening space for BN-PN alliance
- BN Chairman announced BN-PN understanding will continue in Melaka
- SPR (Election Commission) officially announced BN-PN won the 16th Negeri Sembilan State Election (reported the previous day)

### Context
- Statement made after attending IJM Allianz Duo Highway Challenge 2026 running event in Petaling Jaya
- BN-PN cooperation seen as capable of delivering positive results in Melaka PRN
