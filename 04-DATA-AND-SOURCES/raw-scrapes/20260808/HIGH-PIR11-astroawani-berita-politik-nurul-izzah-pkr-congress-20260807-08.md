## PKR Leadership Changes

### Nurul Izzah Takes Leave as PKR Deputy President

- **Nurul Izzah cuti sementara jawatan Timbalan Presiden PKR** — PKR's Central Leadership Council (MPP) agreed to grant Nurul Izzah Anwar temporary leave from her Deputy President role **after the 2026 National Congress in Melaka**
- VP **Saifuddin Nasution Ismail** acting during leave
- (1 day ago = 7 Aug 2026)

### Nurul Izzah Steps Down

- **Hasrat Nurul Izzah lepas jawatan** — She stepped down to focus on further studies
- Expressed gratitude to PKR President Anwar Ibrahim and party leadership for accepting her decision
- (1 day ago = 7 Aug 2026)

### Wikipedia Corroboration

- Nurul Izzah Wikipedia page updated: "Deputy President of PKR: 24 May 2025 – present (**on leave since 16 Aug 2026**)"
- "Succeeded Rafizi Ramli; VP Saifuddin Nasution Ismail acting during leave"
