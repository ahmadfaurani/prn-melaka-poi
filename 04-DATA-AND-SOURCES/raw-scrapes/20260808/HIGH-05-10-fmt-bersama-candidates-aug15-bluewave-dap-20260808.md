## KEY FINDING 4: Opinion — "Bersama, atau bersama-sama" by Faisal Tehrani

**Source:** FMT Opinion, May 21, 2026
**URL:** https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/opinion/2026/05/21/bersama-atau-bersama-sama
**Author:** Faisal Tehrani
**Content:**
- Critiques the BERSAMA party name: "bersama" in Malay tradition refers to bodily/physical relations (sexual connotation); "bersama-sama" is the correct term for cooperation
- Critiques the kancil (mouse deer) logo: in Malay literary tradition, kancil symbolizes cunning, deception, stubbornness — not positive traits
- Historical parallels: political "bersama" (alliances) like PAS+DAP (Pakatan Rakyat), Semangat 46+PAS, PH+Bersatu — all produced mixed results
- Proverb: "Gajah sama gajah bertarung, kancil mati di tengah" — when elephants fight, the kancil dies in the middle
- Author predicts BERSAMA may win some seats (Pandan), but the name is "embarrassing to say" in Malay cultural context
- Notes Rafizi's team did not consult literary/cultural figures when choosing name and logo

**PIR-05/10 Relevance:** Cultural critique of BERSAMA's branding. While opinion (T3), it highlights the challenge BERSAMA faces in Malay-majority Melaka constituencies — the name and logo may not resonate positively with traditional Malay voters. This is relevant to BERSAMA's Melaka electoral prospects.
