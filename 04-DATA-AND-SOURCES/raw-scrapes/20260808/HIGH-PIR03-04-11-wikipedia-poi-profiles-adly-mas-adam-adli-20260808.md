## Adly Zahari (PIR-03)

### Current Positions
- **Deputy Minister of Defence** — since 10 December 2022 (Unity Government under PM Anwar Ibrahim; Minister: Mohamed Khaled Nordin since 2023)
- **MP for Alor Gajah** (P135) — since 19 November 2022 (majority: 890 votes)
- **MLA for Bukit Katil (Malacca)** — since 9 May 2018 (sole Malacca AMANAH MLA)
- **Vice-President of AMANAH** — since 5 September 2021
- **State Chairman of PH (Malacca)** — since 30 August 2017
- **State Chairman of PH (Kelantan)** — since 5 November 2024

### Previous Positions
- 11th Chief Minister of Malacca (11 May 2018 – 9 March 2020) — first-ever transition of power in Malacca
- State Leader of the Opposition (Malacca) (24 April 2020 – 13 December 2022)

### Personal
- Born: 15 February 1971 (age 55), Kampung Pulau, Durian Tunggal, Malacca
- Education: BEng (Electrical Engineering), UTM
- Party: AMANAH (since 2015); formerly PAS (until 2015)

### Key Note
- malaysiacabinet.com lists "Memegang jawatan sejak: 16 Dec 2025" — this refers to page update date (Dec 2025 reshuffle), NOT start of role. Wikipedia confirms Deputy Minister of Defence since 10 December 2022.

## Mas Ermieyati Samsudin (PIR-04)

### Current Positions
- **Chairperson, Public Accounts Committee (PAC)** — since 4 April 2023 (nominated by Anwar Ibrahim; appointed by Johri Abdul; **second female PAC Chairperson** in Malaysian history)
- **MP for Masjid Tanah** (P134) — since 5 May 2013 (incumbent; majorities: 16,200 (2013), 8,159 (2018), 4,411 (2022))
- **Women Chief of BERSATU** — since 28 October 2024 (succeeded Rina Harun)

### BERSATU Status
- Wikipedia table: "2020–present | BERSATU (PN coalition) | Member; **suspended since 2026**"
- This corroborates the 10 March 2026 BERSATU suspension (HIGH-09-astroawani-bersatu-gantung-mas-ermieyati-20260310.md from prior cycle)
- The Feb 2026 resignation was division chief + Srikandi chief; membership status = suspended (not resigned)

### Previous Government Positions
- Deputy Minister in PM's Department (Parliament and Law) — 30 Aug 2021 – 24 Nov 2022 (Ismail Sabri administration)
- Deputy Minister of Entrepreneur Development and Cooperatives — 10 Mar 2020 – 16 Aug 2021 (Muhyiddin administration)
- Deputy Minister of Tourism and Culture — 29 Jul 2015 – 10 May 2018 (Najib administration)

### Personal
- Born: 13 November 1976 (age 49), Malacca General Hospital, Malacca
- Education: Law Degree, UKM (National University of Malaysia)
- Spouse: Mohd Helmy Abd Talib; 5 children
- Career: Co-founded legal firm Messrs. Ermiey Nizam & Ho (2004)

### Political Journey
- UMNO (2001-2018) → Independent (2018) → BERSATU (2019-present, suspended since 2026)
- Among seven UMNO MPs who joined Bersatu (February 2019)

## Adam Adli (PIR-11)

### Current Positions
- **Deputy Minister of Higher Education** — since 16 December 2025 (under PM Anwar Ibrahim; Minister: Zambry Abdul Kadir; preceded Mustapha Sakmud)
- **MP for Hang Tuah Jaya** (P137) — since 19 November 2022 (majority: 8,638; 39,418 votes / 41.72%)
- **Deputy Youth Chief of Pakatan Harapan** — since 29 July 2022
- **Acting Chair of PKR Melaka State Leadership Council (MPN)**
- **Communications Director (PKR)**
- **Central Leadership Council member (PKR)**

### Previous Positions
- Deputy Minister of Youth and Sports — 3 Dec 2022 – 16 Dec 2025 (under Minister Hannah Yeoh)
- 5th Youth Chief of PKR — 17 Jul 2022 – 23 May 2025 (succeeded by Muhammad Kamil Abdul Munim)

### Personal
- Born: 3 July 1989 (age 37), Butterworth, Penang
- Education: Sultan Idris Education University (UPSI)
- Party: PKR (joined 16 September 2021)
- Coalition: Pakatan Harapan (PH)
