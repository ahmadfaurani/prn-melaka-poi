## Part 3: Sinar Daily — BERSAMA Background (May 18, 2026)

**URL:** https://www.sinardaily.my/article/736159/focus/deep-dive/what-is-parti-bersama-malaysia-the-new-third-force-party-led-by-rafizi-nik-nazmi
**Author:** Wan Ahmad Atarmizi
**Date:** May 18, 2026

### Key Facts
- **Official Name:** Parti Bersama Malaysia (BERSAMA) / Malaysian United Party (MUP)
- **Registered:** December 2, 2016, in George Town, Penang
- **Original Founder:** Tan Gin Theam (formerly MCA)
- **New Leaders:** Rafizi Ramli & Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad (took over May 17, 2026)
- **Logo:** "Kancil" (mousedeer) — symbolizing an agile underdog
- **Positioning:** Multiracial centrist party, independent "third force"
- **Linked Figure:** Subang MP Wong Chen

### Rafizi & Nik Nazmi Timeline
1. May 2025: Both lost PKR party elections
2. Removed from federal Cabinet
3. Left PKR altogether
4. May 17, 2026: Officially assumed leadership of BERSAMA at PJ Performing Arts Centre
5. Both vacated parliamentary seats (Pandan, Setiawangsa) to comply with anti-party hopping law

### Election Strategy
- BERSAMA intends to **contest elections independently** — not aligning with existing coalitions
- Strategy focuses on **urban constituencies** and **multiracial voter demographics**
- Key issues: rising cost of living, wage stagnation, education-employment mismatches

**PIR-05/10 Relevance:** BERSAMA's independent positioning means it will NOT join any PH pact. This creates multi-cornered fights in Melaka's urban/mixed seats (DAP's 4 seats: N16 Ayer Keroh, N19 Kesidang, N20 Kota Laksamana, N22 Bandar Hilir). BERSAMA's Aug 15 candidate announcement (per FMT) will reveal which Melaka seats it targets.
