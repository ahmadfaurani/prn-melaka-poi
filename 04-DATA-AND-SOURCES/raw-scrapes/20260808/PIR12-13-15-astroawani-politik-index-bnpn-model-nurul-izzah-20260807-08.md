## 1. BN-PN Seat-Sharing Formula (Negeri Sembilan as Melaka Model)

- **UMNO MKT member Datuk Mohd Razlan Rafii** suggests the BN-PN seat-sharing formula used in Negeri Sembilan could be a **reference model for upcoming elections** to avoid Malay vote split.
- **PIR-12 relevance:** If NS formula applied to Melaka, PAS's 16-seat demand may be negotiated down. Zulkifli's unilateral 16-seat announcement could be reduced through BN-PN negotiation.
- **PIR-13 relevance:** If BN-PN coalition forms in Melaka (like NS), EXCO composition changes. Helmy's Timbalan EXCO role may be affected if PN members join the EXCO.

## 2. First-Ever BN-PN Coalition State Administration (Negeri Sembilan)

- **10 BN-PN ADUNs appointed as Exco** — sworn in at Istana Besar Seri Menanti, marking a historic collaboration.
- **Mentri Besar Datuk Ismail Lasim** leads three key portfolios for 2026-2031 term.
- **Analyst Mujibu warns** of "dua pusat pengaruh" (two centers of influence) risk in BN-PN coalition Exco.
- **PIR-12/13 relevance:** This is the operational template Melaka may follow. The "two centers of influence" risk is a governance warning for Melaka if BN-PN coalition forms.

## 3. PAS-BERSATU-PN Dispute Escalation

- **Hadi Awang** claims BERSATU automatically exited PN
- **Azmin Ali** insists BERSATU is still a legitimate PN member
- **Three senior BERSATU Terengganu leaders resign** — including Datuk Razali Idris, triggering leadership crisis
- **PAS open to receiving BERSATU leaders if they join** (Harakah Daily, Aug 7)
- **PIR-12 relevance:** Post-break PAS strategy includes welcoming individual BERSATU leaders — grassroots outreach beyond coalition politics.
- **PIR-15 relevance:** Bakri's PN Melaka Secretary role further complicated — if BERSATU is out of PN, the structure loses Chairman (Radzi) and Election Director (Ali).

## 4. PKR Leadership Changes (Nurul Izzah Resignation)

- **Nurul Izzah Anwar resigns as PKR Deputy President** to focus on further studies
- **MPP PKR agrees to grant Nurul Izzah temporary leave** from Deputy President role after National Congress 2026 in Melaka next week (Aug 15-16, MITC Melaka)
- **PIR-14 relevance:** Major PKR leadership shakeup. Shamsul Iskandar was already dropped as PKR Melaka Chairman (15 Dec 2025). The Melaka Congress will formalize new PKR leadership. This further marginalizes Shamsul within PKR power structure.

## 5. AMK vs PAS on Tabung Haji RCI

- **AMK (UMNO Youth) criticizes PAS** for practicing "mengalih tiang gol" (shifting goalposts) politics — questioning timing of RCI Tabung Haji report disclosure rather than debating content.
- AMK: "Jika tidak boleh sanggah fakta, jangan main sentimen rakyat" (If you can't refute facts, don't play with people's sentiments)
- **PIR-12/15 relevance:** The 11 Aug special sitting (3 days away) is for Tabung Haji RCI debate. AMK (UMNO Youth, led by Akmal Saleh) attacking PAS = intra-coalition tension between BN and PAS even as they discuss cooperation. Bakri (PAS MP) may attend the sitting.

## 6. PM Anwar in Alor Gajah, Melaka

- PM Anwar: Government allocations for parliamentary constituencies are **NOT the exclusive right** of any single MP.
- Statement made after Friday prayers at Masjid Ar-Rasyidin, Jelatang, **Alor Gajah** (Melaka).
- PM also said "Beza parti tak boleh sekat persahabatan, kerjasama demi manfaat rakyat" (Party differences can't stop friendship, cooperation for people's benefit) — federal-melaka cooperation should be example.
- **PIR context:** Alor Gajah is in Melaka. PM's visit = federal engagement with Melaka state. Relevant to overall PRN Melaka context.

## 7. PRU16 Outlook

- Analyst: PH expected to **strengthen position** post-three state elections
- BN-PN alignment described as **"berliku" (uncertain/winding)**
- PRU16 may be held late 2027
- Activist warns PAS to think deeply before selecting Zahid Hamidi as "poster boy" for PRU16
