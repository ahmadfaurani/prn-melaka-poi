# Abdul Hadi Claims Bersatu Exited PN, Azmin Insists Still Legitimate Member

## Key Dispute

**PAS President Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang** claims Bersatu automatically exited Perikatan Nasional (PN) after expressing intent to form a new coalition, while **Bersatu Secretary General Datuk Seri Mohamed Azmin Ali** firmly rejects this assertion.

---

## Hadi Awang's Position

- **Claim:** Bersatu exited PN automatically following their statement about forming a new coalition
- **Key Quote:**
  > "Dia (Bersatu) sudah membuat kenyataan hendak tubuh satu pasukan lain... automatik dia keluar"
  > (They (Bersatu) have already stated their intention to want to form another team... automatically they are out)
- **Context:** Made after delivering kuliah duha at Masjid Rusila, Marang
- **Stance:** PN need not make formal notification to Bersatu as the party is considered to have exited on its own

## Background Trigger

- **Presiden Bersatu Tan Sri Muhyiddin Yassin** previously announced party would form a new coalition with other parties
- This announcement came after **Negeri Sembilan State Election (PRN) on 1 August**

---

## Azmin Ali's Rebuttal

- **Rejection:** Dismissed Hadi's statement; insists Bersatu remains legitimate PN member
- **Legal Basis:** No provision in PN Constitution allows automatic termination of member party status
- **Key Quote:**
  > "Kedudukan Bersatu sebagai parti anggota Perikatan Nasional hanya boleh dimuktamadkan oleh Majlis Presiden menurut Fasal 7A Perlembagaan Perikatan Nasional. Sehingga kini, Majlis Presiden tidak pernah bersidang untuk menentukan keanggotaan Bersatu dalam Perikatan Nasional"
  > (Bersatu's position as a member party of PN can only be determined by the Presidential Council per Clause 7A of the PN Constitution. To date, the Presidential Council has never convened to determine Bersatu's membership in PN)
- **Veto Power:** Stressed PAS President has **no right or veto power** to determine Bersatu's position in PN
- **Next Step:** Bersatu will file **official complaint with Registrar of Societies (RoS)** regarding this matter

---

## Key Legal/Constitutional Points

| Issue | Position |
|-------|----------|
| Automatic exit provision | **None exists** in PN Constitution (per Azmin) |
| Authority to decide membership | **Majlis Presiden (Presidential Council)** via Clause 7A |
| Presidential Council meetings on this matter | **Never convened** |
| PAS President's authority | **No veto power** over Bersatu's PN membership |
